# Issuing items

**Issue** records equipment handed to one member. Submitting the form creates a
D4H equipment record for each item, in the member's team and held by them, so
you don't have to enter the items in D4H yourself.

You need the **I3 Editor** or **I3 Admin** role, and your own D4H account must be
allowed to create equipment in the member's team.

## Drafts

**Issue** lists your draft forms, newest first. Only you see your drafts. **New**
starts one.

<Screenshot id="d4h-i3/issue-drafts" caption="Your draft Issue forms." />

A draft saves itself as you fill it in, and the form shows **Saved** once it
has. Leave it and come back to it from the list at any time. The bin button at
the bottom of the form discards the draft.

## Filling in the form

You're recorded as the **Issuer**. Pick the **Recipient** first: the list holds
the members of the D4H teams your access token can see, with each one's team.

<Screenshot id="d4h-i3/issue-form" caption="A draft Issue form with three items for one member." />

If your D4H account can't create equipment in the recipient's team, the form
says so and **Submit** stays disabled. Ask whoever manages your D4H account for
the permission, or pick someone from a team you can issue to.

### Adding items

**Add Item** opens once you've picked a recipient. For each item, choose:

- the **Template**, which describes the kind of item (see
  [Templates](/docs/d4h-i3#templates)),
- the **Variant**, such as a size or a model, if the template has variants, and
- the **Serial Number**, if the template needs one. Otherwise the field shows
  N/A.

<Screenshot
  id="d4h-i3/issue-add-item"
  caption="Choosing an item's template, variant and serial number."
/>

The pencil button changes an item and the bin button removes it.

### Templates that fit the recipient

A template's D4H records can belong to your D4H organisation or to one of its
teams. The form only offers templates and variants whose records belong to the
recipient's team or to your D4H organisation, so every item can be created in
that team. If none fit, the dialog says so.

AVUT checks this against D4H when you pick the recipient, and shows **Checking
templates…** until it has. If it can't reach D4H, the form warns you and offers
every template, and **Submit** checks them again.

If you change the recipient after adding items, any item that doesn't fit the
new recipient's team is marked **Can't be issued to** that team. **Submit** stays
disabled until you change or remove it.

## Submitting

Add any **Comments**, then **Submit**. AVUT:

1. checks again that you can create equipment in the recipient's team and that
   every item fits it,
2. creates an equipment record in D4H for each item: in the recipient's team,
   held by the recipient, with the template's category and type, the variant's
   brand and model, and the serial number, and
3. emails a summary to the [notification lists](/docs/d4h-i3#notifications), and
   sends the member a receipt at their D4H email address.

The draft then leaves your list. If the first check fails, nothing is created
and the draft stays, so you can fix it and submit again.

<Callout type="warning">
  If D4H refuses some of the items, AVUT still creates the rest, and doesn't undo them. The
  notification email says how many items weren't recorded. Check the member's equipment in D4H
  before issuing the missing items again, so you don't create them twice.
</Callout>

To take items back, see [Returning items](/docs/d4h-i3/returning).
