# D4H I3

D4H I3 (Issue, Inspect, Return) tracks individually issued items — personal
protective equipment and the like — issued to your members. It works on your D4H
equipment records: issuing an item creates it in D4H, and returning one queues
it to be retired there.

## Before you start

D4H I3 needs:

- the D4H I3 module, the [D4H](/docs/d4h) module and the **D4H Integration**
  turned on in [Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#integrations),
  and
- your own personal D4H access token, added in your account settings under
  **D4H**.

You see the D4H teams your token can see, and what you can do follows your D4H
account's permissions.

## What's here

- **Issue** is where you issue equipment to a member, creating its records in
  D4H. See [Issuing items](/docs/d4h-i3/issuing).
- **Return** is where you take equipment back, queuing a D4H task to retire
  each item. See [Returning items](/docs/d4h-i3/returning).
- **Issued Types** shows what one D4H team has out.
- **Templates** are the items you can issue.

Inspections aren't built yet.

## Notifications

Submitting an Issue or Return form emails a summary to everyone on the
organisation's notification list, and to everyone on the list for the member's
team. The member also gets a receipt at their D4H email address. Returning a
single item from its page sends no emails.

Owners and admins choose who's on these lists in
[Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#d4h-i3), under **Modules** ›
**D4H I3** › **Notifications**: one list for the whole organisation and one for
each team linked to D4H.

## Issued Types

**Issued Types** asks you to pick a D4H team, then shows how many of each type
of equipment the team has out. Open a type to see who holds which items.

## Templates

A **template** describes an item you can issue. Each one is linked to a D4H
equipment **category** and **type**, so the records it creates land in the
right place, and can have variants such as sizes. Deleting a template moves it
to the [Rubbish bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin).

D4H records can belong to your D4H organisation or to one of its teams. When you
pick a category, type, brand or model for a template, the choices are grouped by
who owns them, and on a template's page you can hover over each one to see its
owner. A template using a team's records can only be issued to that team.

### Organisation-owned records only

To keep every template usable by every team, owners and admins can turn on
**Organisation-owned records only** in
[Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#d4h-i3). The template pickers
then offer only your D4H organisation's records, and existing templates that use
a team's records get a **Not organisation records** badge, so you can find and
change them.

## Roles

- An **I3 Editor** can issue and return items.
- An **I3 Admin** can also create, change and delete templates.

Owners, Admins and both I3 roles can also edit items and work tasks in the
[D4H](/docs/d4h#roles) module.
