# Returning items

**Return** records equipment a member hands back. D4H can't retire an item
through its API, so a return doesn't change D4H by itself. Instead, each
returned item gets a [D4H task](/docs/d4h#tasks) to retire it, for someone to
finish in D4H.

You need the **I3 Editor** or **I3 Admin** role, and your own D4H account must be
allowed to update equipment in the member's team.

## Drafts

Return forms are drafts, the same as [Issue forms](/docs/d4h-i3/issuing#drafts):
**Return** lists yours, **New** starts one, a draft saves itself as you fill it
in, and the bin button discards it.

## Filling in the form

Pick the member handing the items back under **Returned by**. Their issued items
are listed by type, each with its ref and any serial number. Items already
returned, and waiting for their task to be finished in D4H, aren't listed.

<Screenshot
  id="d4h-i3/return-form"
  caption="A draft Return form, with three of the member's items ticked."
/>

Tick each item they're handing back. Picking a different member clears the
ticks, since the list is now someone else's items.

Add any **Comments**. You're recorded as **Received by**.

If your D4H account can't update equipment in the member's team, the form says
so and **Submit** stays disabled.

## Submitting

**Submit** queues a task for each ticked item to retire it in D4H, with the
reason "Returned To Cache". The task's note says it was returned through AVUT and
who received it, followed by your comments. The item shows a **Returned** badge
until the task is closed.

AVUT then emails a summary to the
[notification lists](/docs/d4h-i3#notifications), and sends the member a
receipt, listing the items returned, at their D4H email address.

AVUT looks each item up in D4H first, and skips it if:

- it isn't in the member's team any more,
- the member no longer holds it,
- it's already retired, or
- it already has an open task to retire it.

The other items are still returned, and the message after you submit lists the
skipped ones and why. If no item could be returned, nothing is queued or
emailed, and the draft stays so you can fix it.

## Returning a single item

To return one item without a form, open its [item page](/docs/d4h#item-page) and
choose **Return** from the **Actions** menu. The dialog shows who holds the item
and that you're receiving it, with optional comments.

<Screenshot id="d4h-i3/return-item-dialog" caption="Returning one item from its page." />

**Return** queues the same task as the form does. It doesn't email the
notification lists or send the member a receipt.

## Finishing the return in D4H

The queued tasks are listed under **D4H** › [Tasks](/docs/d4h#tasks). Retire
each item in D4H, then press **Check** on its task: AVUT confirms the change in
D4H and closes the task.
