# Bundles

A **bundle** is a named list of [templates](/docs/d4h-i3#templates) that go
together, such as the kit a new member is issued ("Recruit") or what every
responder should carry ("Responder"). Compare a bundle with a D4H team to see,
for each member, which of its items they hold and which they're missing.

## Creating a bundle

**Bundles** lists your organisation's bundles. **New Bundle** asks for a
**Name** and an optional **Description**.

A bundle's page shows its details and a **Templates** card, listing each
template with its category. The **+** button on the card opens **Add Templates**,
where you tick the templates to add, grouped by category, and the bin button on a row
removes one. A bundle is just a set of templates, with no order or quantities.

A template is flagged on the card if it's been deleted, or has no D4H type. Both
are left out of comparisons.

**Edit** and **Delete** are in the page's **Actions** menu. Deleting a bundle
moves it to the [Rubbish bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin), and its page then
offers **Recover from rubbish** in the same menu.

The bundle page reads nothing from D4H, so you can build bundles without a D4H
access token.

## Comparing with a team

**Compare with a team** on a bundle's page asks you to pick a D4H team, then
shows a matrix of the team's members against the bundle's templates, grouped by
category. Each member's row counts what they hold, such as "5 of 7", and each
template's column counts how many members hold it.

Comparing reads D4H, so it needs your [D4H access token](/docs/account#d4h-access-token), and
the **I3 Editor** or **I3 Admin** role.

- **Held** means the member has an item of the template's D4H type, in any
  size. Only items the team owns count.
- **Missing** means they don't. An item marked Lost doesn't count as held.

A held item can still be flagged in amber, by the same checks as
[Issued Types](/docs/d4h-i3#checking-issued-items): **Incomplete** if a part
the template requires is blank, or **Doesn't match** if its brand and model
aren't one of the template's variants. Both still count as held. A legend above
the matrix explains the marks.

Some templates can't be compared, and are listed above the matrix as left out:
deleted ones, ones with no D4H type, and ones whose type the team doesn't use.

### One member

Open a member from the matrix to see their items against the bundle, by
category. Each template shows **Held**, **Incomplete**, **Doesn't match** or
**Missing**, with the items behind it. Under a missing template, any of the
member's items marked Lost are listed.

A bundle doesn't know who it applies to, so whether a gap matters is up to you:
a new member may not have been issued everything yet.

## Roles

- An **I3 Admin** creates, changes and deletes bundles.
- An **I3 Editor** can view and compare them.
