# Team spaces

A team can have its own **space** in AVUT, at `/teams/<slug>`, next to your
organisation, your personal account and the system area. It's a place for the
people who run a team to see and look after it, without going through the
organisation's **Admin**.

## Getting there

A team has a space once an owner or admin gives it a **slug** in
[Admin › Teams](/docs/admin/teams#slug-and-access). You can open it once you've
been given **access** to it, on the team's **Access** card. Being an admin, or
being on the team's roster, doesn't give access on its own.

The teams you have access to are listed under **Teams** in the
[scope switcher](/docs/getting-started/controls#scope-switcher) at the top of the
sidebar. If you belong to more than one organisation, each team's organisation
is named beside it.

## The team's dashboard

A team's space opens on its dashboard:

- **Roster**: the active people on the team.
- **About**: its organisation and description.
- **D4H**: the D4H team it's linked to, and that team's server. This only shows
  when your organisation uses D4H.

## Team Admin

**Admin**, in the team space's sidebar, is where people with access look after
the team.

- The **Team** card shows its name, slug and description. Change them with
  **Edit**, from the card's pencil or the **Actions** menu. You can change the
  slug, which moves the space to the new address, but not clear it, since a
  team needs a slug to have a space.
- **Access** lists who can use the space. Grant access with **+**, and revoke
  it from a user's row. If you revoke your own, you're warned first, then taken
  out of the space.
- **Linked D4H Team** shows the D4H team it follows, once it's linked. With the
  D4H integration on, link the team, sync its members or unlink it from the
  **Actions** menu, the same way as in
  [Admin › Teams](/docs/admin/teams#linking-a-team-to-d4h).
- **Members** lists the team's roster. **New Member** adds someone, and the
  remove button on a row takes them off.

What you can change still follows your roles in the organisation, the same as
in Admin › Teams. Archiving or deleting the team, and clearing its slug, are
only done from the organisation's **Admin** › **Teams**.
