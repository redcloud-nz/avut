# Controls

A handful of controls and icons appear all over AVUT, not tied to any one
module. This page is a quick reference for what they look like and what they
do.

## Theme switcher

<DocsIcon name="sun" /> <DocsIcon name="moon" />

In the sidebar footer, opens a menu to pick **Light**, **Dark**, or
**System** — System follows your device's setting.

## Scope switcher

<Screenshot id="getting-started/scope-switcher" />

At the top of the sidebar, shows where you currently are — an organisation,
your **Personal Account**, or **System** — and opens a menu to jump straight
to any organisation you belong to, your personal account, or (if you have
the access) the system admin area.

## User menu

<Screenshot id="getting-started/user-menu" />

Your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar opens a menu to your account
settings and sign-out. Switching between organisations, your personal
account, or the system admin area is the scope switcher's job — see above.

## Sidebar open/close

<DocsIcon name="panel-left" />

Collapses or expands the sidebar. On a phone, the sidebar opens as an
overlay instead of sitting alongside the page.

## The pencil icon

<DocsIcon name="pencil" />

Marks something as editable — click it (or the row/field it's attached to)
to make a change.

## The vertical ellipsis (⋮)

<DocsIcon name="more-vertical" />

Opens a menu of further actions for whatever it's attached to — a row, a
record, a control. It's the app-wide "more options" button.

## Table header menus

<DocsIcon name="chevron-down" />
<DocsIcon name="sort-asc" />
<DocsIcon name="sort-desc" />
<DocsIcon name="filter" />

Click a column header to sort or filter by that column. Once a sort or
filter is applied, the header shows which one — an arrow for sort direction,
a filter icon when values are filtered.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Many pages support keyboard shortcuts for common actions. Press <Keys>?</Keys>
anywhere to open a dialogue listing every shortcut available on the page you're
on. Shortcuts are shown like this: <Keys combo="mod+k" />.
