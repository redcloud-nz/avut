# Getting Started

AVUT is organised around **organisations**. When you sign in you land on the
dashboard for your current organisation; if you belong to more than one, open
the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Switch Organisation**.

## Modules

Each organisation turns on the **modules** it needs. A module is a self-contained
area of the app — equipment tracking, skill checks, notes, and so on. Only the
modules your organisation has enabled appear in the sidebar.

Every organisation has the **Admin** module, which is where owners and admins
manage people, teams, and invitations.

## Navigating

- The **sidebar** lists the modules available to you. Collapse it with the
  toggle in the top-left.
- **Breadcrumbs** at the top of each page show where you are and let you step
  back up.
- The **help button** (`?`) in the top bar opens the guide for the page you're
  on, without leaving it.

## Getting help

If a guide doesn't answer your question, contact your organisation's owner or
the AVUT team.
