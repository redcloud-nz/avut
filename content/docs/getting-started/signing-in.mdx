# Signing in

AVUT uses email-and-password sign-in. Go to the sign-in page, enter the email
address you were invited with, and your password.

## First time

If someone has invited you to an organization, you'll get an email with a link
to accept the invitation. Follow it, set a password, and you're in.

## Forgot your password

Use the **Forgot password?** link on the sign-in page. You'll get an email with
a link to choose a new one. The link expires after a short time — request a
fresh one if it's stopped working.

## Verifying your email

Some actions require a verified email address. If you see a prompt to verify,
check your inbox for the verification message and follow its link.
