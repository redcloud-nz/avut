# Signing in

You can sign in to AVUT with an **email address and password**, or with
**social sign-in** (Google or GitHub). Every account must have a **verified
email address** — you can't get into the app until yours is verified.

<Screenshot
  id="getting-started/sign-in"
  caption="The sign-in screen — email and password, or continue with Google or GitHub."
/>

## First time

If someone has invited you to an organization, you'll get an email with a link
to accept the invitation. Follow it, set a password (or continue with social
sign-in), and you're in.

## Verifying your email

When you sign up, AVUT emails you a **one-time code**. Enter it to verify your
address. Until you do, sign-in won't let you through — if you try to sign in
with an unverified address, AVUT sends a fresh code and asks you to enter it.

<Screenshot
  id="getting-started/verify-email"
  caption="Enter the 6-digit code from your email. Use Resend if it doesn't arrive."
/>

## Forgot your password

Use the **Forgot password?** link on the sign-in page and enter your email
address.

<Screenshot id="getting-started/forgot-password" />

AVUT emails you a **one-time code**; enter it, then choose a new password. The
code expires after a short time — request a fresh one if it's stopped working.

<Screenshot
  id="getting-started/reset-password"
  caption="Enter the code and your new password to finish resetting it."
/>
