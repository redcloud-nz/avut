# Organisation

## Name and slug

**Organisation** shows your organisation's name, slug and ID. Owners and admins
change the name and slug with **Edit**.

The slug is the part of the address after `/orgs/`. Changing it changes every
link to your organisation, so bookmarks and links people have saved stop
working.

## D4H Integration

Once a team has been [linked to D4H](/docs/admin/teams#linking-a-team-to-d4h),
owners and admins also see a **D4H Integration** card here, with the D4H
organisation, its server and when it was last synced.

- **Sync with D4H** refreshes those details. It's available while at least one
  team is linked.
- **Unlink from D4H** only works once no team is linked: unlink each team
  first.

## Organisation Settings

Everyone in the organisation can view **Organisation Settings**; only owners
and admins can change them.

### General

**Time zone** is the zone your organisation operates in. Skill check
[session](/docs/skill-track/sessions) start and end times are entered and shown
in it, whatever time zone each person has chosen for themselves.

### Personnel

Two switches decide when AVUT links a person record to a member's account by
matching email — see [Users vs Personnel](/docs/admin/user-vs-person#how-they-connect):

- **Link on invitation accept** — when someone accepts an invitation, link them
  to the person record with the same email, if there is one.
- **Link when a person is added** — when a person is added, link them to the
  existing member with the same email. Someone who isn't already a member is
  never added to the organisation; invite them from their person record
  instead.

### Rubbish Bin

**Keep deleted records for** sets how many days deleted records stay in the
[Rubbish bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin) before they're purged: 1 to 90 days, 30
by default.

### Integrations

**D4H Integration** turns on D4H for the organisation, with its default
server. Each member who uses D4H still adds their own personal access token in
their account settings under **D4H**. The [D4H](/docs/d4h) and
[D4H I3](/docs/d4h-i3) modules and
[team linking](/docs/admin/teams#linking-a-team-to-d4h) all need it.

### Modules

Each module has its own heading, with a short description of what it's for.
Turn a module on or off with **Enable** and **Disable**. Turning a module off
hides it for everyone, and stops its roles being offered, but deletes nothing:
turn it back on and everything is where you left it. Admin is always on.

Some modules need another one: D4H I3 can only be turned on while D4H is.

#### D4H I3

- **Organisation-owned records only** — offer only your D4H organisation's
  records when creating or editing I3 templates, so every template can be issued
  in every team. See [Templates](/docs/d4h-i3#organisation-owned-records-only).
- **Notifications** — who is emailed when items are issued or returned: one list
  for the whole organisation, and one for each team linked to D4H. Edit the
  lists with the pencil button. See [Notifications](/docs/d4h-i3#notifications).

#### Skill Track

**Skill Check Result Options** sets which results your organisation can record,
and what each one is called — see
[Skill Track](/docs/skill-track#skill-checks).
