# Rubbish Bin

Deleting a record in AVUT doesn't remove it straight away; it moves to the
Rubbish bin. **Rubbish**, on the Admin page, lists what's there, most recently
deleted first.

## What it holds

- Personnel
- Teams, and team memberships (a person removed from a team)
- I3 templates
- Skill packages, skill groups and skills

You only see the kinds of record you're allowed to delete, and the **Rubbish**
link only shows if you can delete at least one of these kinds.

Not everything goes to the bin. Deleting a user, a note or a skill check
session is permanent straight away.

## Recover or delete forever

Each row has two buttons:

- **Recover** puts the record back as it was.
- **Delete forever** removes it, and everything that belongs to it, once you
  confirm. This can't be undone. Deleting a person forever also deletes the
  skill checks recorded about them.

## When records are purged

The **Purges** column shows when each record will be removed automatically.
Records are kept for 30 days, unless your organisation has changed that under
**Rubbish Bin** in [Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#rubbish-bin)
(1 to 90 days). **Not scheduled** means the deletion date isn't known, so the
record is never purged automatically.

<Callout>
  A record can be held back from being deleted for good — for example, a skill that another
  organisation has recorded checks against. It stays in the bin, and **Delete forever** refuses too,
  until that changes.
</Callout>
