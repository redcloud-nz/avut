# Teams

A **team** groups your personnel. Other modules use teams too: Skill Track
sessions pick their personnel by team, and its reports can be scoped to one.

Everyone in the organisation can view teams. Owners and admins make the changes
described below.

## Teams and members

The Teams list shows **Active** teams. **New Team** adds one.

A team's page shows its details, and its **Related** card counts the personnel
on it. Open that for the member list, where **New Member** adds someone. **Add
person**, in the team's menu, does the same.

Open a member to see the membership itself. **Remove from team** takes them off
the team and moves the membership to the [Rubbish bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin),
so it can be recovered. The person record isn't touched.

## Archiving and deleting

The team's menu (⋮) has **Edit**, **Add person**, **Archive** and **Delete**.

- **Archive** keeps the team's members and history, but the team takes no new
  members and stops syncing with D4H. Personnel on no other active team can't be
  added to a Skill Track session. **Restore** undoes it.
- **Delete** moves the team to the Rubbish bin, where it can be recovered until
  it's purged.

**History** lists every change made to the team, and who made it.

## Linking a team to D4H

With the [D4H integration](/docs/admin/organization#integrations) on, owners and
admins can keep a team's members in step with a team in D4H. Linking uses your
own personal D4H access token (**User Settings → D4H**), so you can only link to
a D4H team that token can see.

- **Link to D4H**, on an active team, picks the D4H team. **Link & sync** links
  it and imports its members straight away. Each D4H member is matched to a
  person record by email; anyone without one gets a new record.
- **Sync with D4H**, on the team's **D4H Integration** card, fetches the D4H
  team and previews what would change before anything does: members to
  **Add**, **Update**, **Reactivate** or **Archive**, and changes to the team's
  own details. **Apply** makes the changes; **Cancel** leaves everything as it
  is. If nothing has changed, it says **Already in sync**.
- **Unlink from D4H** stops the syncing. The personnel and memberships it
  imported stay.

Members imported from D4H carry a **D4H** badge in the member list. On a linked
team, people you add by hand carry **Manual**, and syncing leaves them alone.

<Callout>
  A D4H member with no email address in D4H can't be matched, so the sync skips them and lists them.
  So does a member whose email belongs to a person in the Rubbish bin: recover that person, or
  delete them forever, then sync again.
</Callout>

Once any team has been linked, the organisation's **D4H Integration** card
appears on the [Organisation](/docs/admin/organization#d4h-integration) page.
