# Users

**Users** lists your organisation's members: the accounts that can sign in to
it, the person record each one is linked to, and the roles each one holds.

You can't add a user here. Someone becomes a user by accepting an invitation,
sent from [Invitations](/docs/admin/invitations) or with **Invite to AVUT** on
their [person record](/docs/admin/personnel).

## A user's page

Open a user to see their details. Owners and admins can use its menu (⋮) to:

- **Edit** their roles. Tick any combination; see [Roles](/docs/admin#roles)
  for what each allows. A role that another ticked role already includes shows
  ticked and greyed out, and a module's roles only appear while that module is
  turned on.
- **Link person** or **Unlink** their person record — see
  [Users vs Personnel](/docs/admin/user-vs-person). Some screens, such as
  recording a skill check, need you to have a linked person.
- **Delete** them — see below.

## Owners

**Make owner** and **Remove owner**, in the same menu, grant and take away
ownership. Only an owner can use them, and you can't remove your own ownership,
so the organisation always keeps an owner.

Owners can do everything an admin can, plus manage other owners.

## Deleting a user

**Delete** removes someone from your organisation straight away. It doesn't go
to the Rubbish bin; to bring them back, invite them again.

Deleting only ends their membership. Their sign-in account is kept, along with
any other organisations they belong to, and so is their person record here,
which is just unlinked.

You can't delete yourself, and only an owner can delete another owner.
