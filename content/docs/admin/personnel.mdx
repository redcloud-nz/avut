# Personnel

**Personnel** holds a person record for everyone your organisation tracks:
members, volunteers, anyone you want on a team or assessed in Skill Track. A
person doesn't need a login. Link them to a user account only if they'll sign
in — see [Users vs Personnel](/docs/admin/user-vs-person).

Everyone in the organisation can view Personnel. Owners and admins make the
changes described below.

## Adding someone

**New Person** asks for a name and an email address, and opens the new record.
If **Link when a person is added** is on in **Organisation Settings**, and the
email belongs to an existing member, the record is linked to their account
straight away.

## A person's page

A person's page shows their details, the teams they're on and any linked user
account. Its menu (⋮) has:

- **Edit** — change their name or email.
- **Add to team** — put them on one of your teams.
- **Invite to AVUT** — email them an invitation to sign in. Accepting it links
  their new account to this record. If the email already belongs to a member,
  it offers **Link Account** instead.
- **Link to user** / **Unlink from user** — connect an existing member's
  account, or disconnect it. Neither record is deleted.
- **Archive** / **Restore** — see below.
- **Delete** — see below.

**History** lists every change made to the record, and who made it.

## Archiving

Archive someone who has left but whose record you want to keep. Their teams and
history stay, but they drop out of the **Active** list and out of the pickers
and reports that only show active personnel. **Restore** brings them back.

The Personnel list shows active personnel to begin with. Change the **Status**
filter to see archived ones.

## Deleting

**Delete** moves the record to the [Rubbish bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin). Until
it's purged, **Recover** puts it back as it was, and so does **Recover from
rubbish** in the record's own menu.

When a person is purged, from the Rubbish bin or automatically, the skill checks
recorded _about_ them are deleted with them. Checks they recorded as an
assessor on other people are kept.
