# D4H Views

D4H Views shows data from **D4H** inside AVUT, read-only, so you can look up
equipment and members without switching tools. Nothing here changes D4H; make
changes in D4H itself.

## Requirements

D4H Views uses **your own personal D4H access token**, not one shared by the
organisation, so you see what your D4H account can see. Add a token under
**User Settings → D4H**.

The organisation also needs the D4H Views module and the **D4H Integration**
turned on in [Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#integrations).

## What you can see

- **Equipment** — browse by **Equipment Brands** and their models, or by
  **Equipment Categories** and their kinds, down to individual items.
- **Members** — the D4H members of your teams.
