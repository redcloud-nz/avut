# D4H Attendance Reminders

D4H Attendance Reminders emails the members of a team who haven't answered a
D4H exercise invitation yet: those whose attendance is still **Requested**. You
set when it runs, team by team, and it takes the chasing off team leaders.

Only owners and admins see the module.

## Before you start

The module needs:

- the **D4H Integration** and the [D4H](/docs/d4h) module turned on, then
  **D4H Attendance Reminders** itself, in
  [Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#modules),
- an organisation D4H access token that can see the teams you want reminded,
  added under **Admin** › [D4H Access Tokens](/docs/admin/organization#d4h-access-tokens)
  and chosen as the module's **D4H Token** in its settings, and
- each team you want reminded [linked to its D4H team](/docs/admin/teams#linking-a-team-to-d4h).

The reminders run on their own, with no one signed in, so they use the
organisation's token rather than anyone's personal one. A read-only token is
enough.

## Rules

**Rules** lists your reminder rules. A rule says which team to remind, and
when:

- **Team**: one of your teams linked to D4H. Its D4H team's members are the
  ones reminded.
- **Day** and **Time**: when the rule runs each week, in your organisation's
  time zone.
- **Look ahead**: how many hours ahead to look for exercises, 24 by default.
  Exercises starting less than an hour away are left out, since a reminder then
  would be too late to help.
- **Message**: an optional note added to the email.

For example, a rule for Sundays at 18:00 that looks 72 hours ahead reminds
members about exercises from Sunday evening to Wednesday evening. A team can
have several rules.

**New Rule** adds one. Open a rule to edit, disable or delete it. Deleting a
rule keeps its past runs.

### Paused rules

The **Status** column shows **Enabled** for a rule that will run. One that can't
says why instead, for example **Team not
linked to D4H**, **No D4H token** or **Token can't see team**. It's skipped
until you fix the cause.

## Runs

Each time a rule runs, it records a **run**. A rule's page lists its runs. Open
one to see the exercises it found, and each member it considered with the
**Outcome**:

- **Sent**: the reminder went out.
- **Skipped: already reminded**: they've already been reminded about that
  exercise, by this rule or another. Each member is reminded once per exercise.
- **Skipped: opted out**: they've asked not to get reminders.
- **Skipped: no email**: their D4H record has no email address.
- **Failed**: the email couldn't be sent. A later run tries again.

Besides its schedule, you can start a rule from its **Run** menu:

- **Run Now** sends reminders straight away, looking ahead from now.
- **Dry Run** works out who would be reminded, marking them **Would send**,
  without sending anything.

A run's page updates as it goes. Runs are kept for 90 days.

## The email

The reminder names the exercise and when it starts, includes your rule's
message, and asks the member to update their attendance in D4H, with a button
to open the exercise there. It comes from AVUT, with replies going to your
organisation's [Reply-To address](/docs/admin/organization#general), so set one.

Every reminder ends with a link to stop them. It opens a page where the member
can **Stop sending me these reminders**, or later **Send me these reminders
again**. Opting out covers every rule for that D4H team.

## Opt-outs

**Opt-outs** lists the members who've stopped their reminders, and whether
they did it themselves or an admin did. **Add Opt-out** stops them for a member
of a team's D4H team, for someone who's asked you directly. Remove an opt-out
to start their reminders again.
