# Your Account

Your account is separate from any one organisation — the same login can belong
to several. Manage it from the **User Settings** group in the menu under your
name: **Account**, **Organisations**, **D4H** and **Preferences**.

## What you can change

- **Profile** — your name and the email address used for sign-in and
  notifications.
- **Password** — set a new one at any time. You'll stay signed in on this
  device.
- **Organisations** — see every organisation you belong to and the role you
  hold in each. Leaving an organisation removes your access to it immediately.
- **Preferences** — how dates and times are shown to you: the date format, the
  time format and your time zone. Every date in AVUT follows them.

## D4H access token

If your organisation uses [D4H](/docs/glossary#d4h), the D4H-backed parts of
AVUT — [D4H Views](/docs/d4h-views), [I3](/docs/i3) and
[linking a team to D4H](/docs/admin/teams#linking-a-team-to-d4h) — use your own
personal D4H access token, so you see what your D4H account can see. Add one
under **User Settings → D4H** with **Add Personal D4H Access Token**, and remove
it from the token's own page. Create the token in D4H first.

## Closing your account

**Close Account**, on the **Account** page, signs you out everywhere and removes
your access to every organisation. Your account is permanently deleted after 14
days; until then, signing in again restores it. The person records your
organisations keep about you are theirs, and aren't removed.

If you're the only owner of an organisation, the dialog warns you that it will
be left with no owner.

## Notifications

AVUT emails you about invitations and a few account-security events. These
aren't optional — they're tied to keeping your account usable and secure.
