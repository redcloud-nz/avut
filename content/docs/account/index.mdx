# Your Account

Your account is separate from any one organization — the same login can belong
to several. Manage it from **User Settings**, reached from the menu under your
name.

## What you can change

- **Profile** — your name and the email address used for sign-in and
  notifications.
- **Password** — set a new one at any time. You'll stay signed in on this
  device.
- **Organizations** — see every organization you belong to and the role you
  hold in each. Leaving an organization removes your access to it immediately.

## Notifications

AVUT emails you about invitations and a few account-security events. These
aren't optional — they're tied to keeping your account usable and secure.
