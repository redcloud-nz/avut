# D4H

The D4H module shows data from **D4H** inside AVUT, so you can look up teams,
members and equipment without switching tools. Most of it is for looking up;
the exceptions are editing an item and working through D4H tasks.

## Requirements

D4H uses **your own personal D4H access token**, not one shared by the
organisation, so you see what your D4H account can see. Add a token in your
account settings under **D4H**.

The organisation also needs the **D4H Integration** and the D4H module turned on
in [Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#integrations).

## Teams and members

- **Teams** lists the D4H teams you can see. A team's page links to its
  **Members**.
- **Members** asks you to pick a team, then lists that team's members. Use
  **Change team** to see another team's. A member's page shows the equipment
  issued to them.

## Equipment

Equipment is organised two ways in D4H, and both cover every team you can see.
Each list has an **Owner** column for the team or organisation each record
belongs to.

- **Equipment Categories** — a category's page lists its **types**, and a type's
  page lists its items in every team, with who holds each one.
- **Equipment Brands** — a brand's page lists its models.

Every team, member, item, category, type, brand and model name links to its
page, here and in [D4H I3](/docs/d4h-i3).

### Item page

An item's page shows one piece of equipment: its details, its **Parents** and
**Contents**, and any open D4H tasks for it. **Open in D4H** shows the item in D4H
itself. The **Actions** menu has:

- **Edit**, which changes the item in D4H: its status, whether it's critical or
  monitored, its barcode and its notes.
- **Return**, when [D4H I3](/docs/d4h-i3/returning#returning-a-single-item) is
  turned on and the item is issued to someone.

## Tasks

Some changes can't be made through D4H's API, such as retiring an item. AVUT
queues these as **D4H tasks** for someone to make by hand. **Tasks** lists the
open ones, each with the change it needs.

Make the change in D4H, then press **Check**: AVUT looks the item up in D4H and
closes the task once the change is there. **Dismiss** closes a task without
checking, for one that no longer applies.

## Roles

Anyone in the organisation can look up teams, members and equipment. Editing
items and **Tasks** need the Owner, Admin, I3 Editor or I3 Admin role. **Edit** also needs
your own D4H account to be able to update equipment in the item's team.
