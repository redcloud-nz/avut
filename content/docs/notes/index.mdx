# Notes

Notes is a simple notebook — meeting minutes, running checklists, reference
material, anything worth keeping in one place. There are two sets of notes:

- **Notes** in your organisation's sidebar are shared with its members. The
  organisation needs the Notes module turned on in **Organisation Settings**.
- **Notes** in your own account (your **Personal Account**, from the scope
  switcher) are personal: only you can see them, whichever organisations you
  belong to.

Both work the same way.

## Finding a note

The list shows each note's title, when it was last updated and who wrote it.
Sort it by **Last updated**, **Created** or **Title**. Click a note to open it
beside the list. On a phone, the note opens on its own, and **Notes** in the
breadcrumb takes you back to the list.

## Writing a note

**New note** creates a note called **Untitled note** and opens it for editing,
with the title selected so you can type over it.

To change a note, click the pencil (**Edit note**). The title and body become
editable, with a formatting toolbar for headings, lists, links and the like.
Nothing is saved until you click **Save**; **Cancel** drops your changes.

## History and deleting

The note's menu (⋮) has **History**, which lists every change made to the note
and who made it, and **Delete**. Deleting is permanent: a note doesn't go to the
Rubbish bin.

## Who can do what

In an organisation, every member can read its notes and add their own. You can
edit or delete the notes you wrote. Owners and admins can edit or delete
anyone's.
