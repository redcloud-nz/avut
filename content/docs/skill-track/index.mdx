# Skill Track

Skill Track records who has been assessed on which skills, and when. It covers
running assessment sessions, the skill catalogue your organization works from,
and the reports that summarize competency across your people.

## Key ideas

- The **catalogue** is the set of skill packages available to your
  organization.
- A **skill package** is a collection of skills and groups that defines what can
  be assessed.
- A **skill check** is a single record that a person was assessed on a skill,
  with an outcome and a date.
- A **session** groups the checks done together — for example, one assessor
  working through a group of people on a training day.
- **Reports** roll checks up into a matrix so you can see gaps at a glance.

## Roles

Assessing requires the `skills-assessor` role. Everyday members can view their
own results and the reports their role allows.
