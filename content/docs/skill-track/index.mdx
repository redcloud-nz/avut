# Skill Track

Skill Track records who has been assessed on which skills, and when. It covers
the skill catalogue your organisation works from, running assessment sessions,
one-off checks outside a session, and the reports that summarise competency
across your people.

## What's here

- **Assessors** — who holds the Skills Assessor role. See [Roles](#roles).
- **[Catalogue](/docs/skill-track/catalogue)** — the skill packages on offer,
  and the ones your organisation subscribes to.
- **[Checks](/docs/skill-track/checks)** — every check from the last month,
  and recording one outside a session.
- **[Sessions](/docs/skill-track/sessions)** — the usual way to run
  assessments: pick who and what, record results, then review and approve.
- **[Reports](/docs/skill-track/reports)** — competency and currency, viewed
  by person, by team, by skill, or as one big matrix.

You only see the links your roles allow.

## Skill checks

A **skill check** is a single record that someone was assessed on a skill,
with a result and a date. The possible results are:

- **Fail tiers** — Low Fail, Fail, High Fail
- **Pass tiers** — Weak Pass, Pass, Strong Pass
- **Other statuses** — Not Taught, Exempt, Expired, Provisional

Only the three pass tiers count as competent — everything else, including
Fail and Not Taught, means the person isn't yet competent in that skill (see
[Reports](/docs/skill-track/reports) for how that rolls up).

Your organisation can turn individual results on or off and rename them, from
**Admin → Organisation Settings → Skill Track Module → Skill Check Result
Options**. Turning a result off only hides it from the recording screens — it
doesn't delete anything already recorded against it.

<Callout>
  Exempt, Expired, and Provisional aren't configurable yet — only the other seven results can be
  toggled and renamed today.
</Callout>

## Roles

Skill Track has its own roles, which an owner or admin grants under **Admin →
Users** (see [Roles](/docs/admin#roles)):

- A **Skills Assessor** runs sessions and records checks.
- A **Skills Reporter** can view checks, sessions and reports, but not change
  them.
- A **Skills Admin** can do everything an assessor and a reporter can, and
  also approve, reopen and delete sessions, and subscribe to skill packages.

Writing skill packages is a separate role, **Skills Author**, in the
[Skill Package Builder](/docs/skill-package-builder).

The **Assessors** page lists your organisation's members with a **Skills
Assessor** switch for each. Owners, admins and Skills Admins use it to hand out
the role without editing anyone's other roles. A member whose only role is
Skills Assessor shows **Their only role** instead of a switch, and a Skills Admin
already has everything an assessor does.

Members without any Skill Track role only see the catalogue. Only checks in an
approved session, or added outside any session, count in reports.
