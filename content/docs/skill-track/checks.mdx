# Checks

**Skill Checks** lists every check recorded in your organisation, newest first,
from sessions and from outside them, with its result, session, date and
assessor. A session's checks show here whether or not the session has been
approved. Long lists are split into pages.

Skills Assessors and Skills Admins can add checks. Skills Reporters can view the
list.

## Finding checks

The **Filters** card above the list narrows it down:

- **Person**: the person checked.
- **Skill**.
- **Session**, or **No session** for checks added outside one.
- **Assessor**.
- **Result**.
- **Date**: a range of days, in your own time zone, the same as the list's
  **Date** column.

Each filter has its own clear button. The card's heading sums up the filters in
a sentence, ending in **Clear filters**, so you can collapse the card and still
see what the list shows.

The filters are part of the page's address, so you can bookmark a filtered view
or share its link. A skill's page links here as **Recent checks**, under
**Related**, already filtered to that skill. See
[Packages and skills](/docs/skill-track/packages#skills).

## Adding a check outside a session

**Add Check** records a single check outside any session — an informal
observation, say, that you want on record without setting up a whole session,
or a result from before your organisation used AVUT. Pick the person, the skill
and a result, and add notes if you like.

**Date checked** is today unless you pick an earlier day. Reports date the
check, and work out when it expires, from that day.

You're always recorded as the assessor, so you need a
[person record linked](/docs/admin/user-vs-person) to your account.

A check added here belongs to no session, so there's no review or approval: it
counts in the [reports](/docs/skill-track/reports) as soon as you add it. When
a result should be reviewed before it counts, record it in a
[session](/docs/skill-track/sessions) instead.
