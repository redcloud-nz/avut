# Sessions

A **skill check session** is the usual way to run assessments — for example,
everything assessed on one training night. You choose who and what is being
assessed, one or more assessors record the results, and a Skills Admin reviews
and approves the session. Only checks in an approved session count in
[reports](/docs/skill-track/reports), along with any added outside a session.

Skills Assessors and Skills Admins run sessions. Skills Reporters can open them
and see everything, but not change anything. Approving, reopening and deleting a
session is for Skills Admins.

## 1. Create the session

Start from the Sessions list with **New Session**, giving it a name, a date and
optional notes. All three are optional: leave the name blank and it takes the
next session number shown in the placeholder, and leave the date and it
defaults to today.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/create-session"
  caption="Leave the name blank to take the suggested session number, and the date to take today."
/>

Every check recorded in the session is dated with the session's date, not the
day it was entered. Set it to the day the assessing happened. If you change the
date later, the session's checks move with it.

Creating a session makes you its first and only assessor, so you need a
[person record linked](/docs/admin/user-vs-person) to your account.

## Getting around a session

Creating a session drops you straight onto its own page, and every step that
follows starts from there. To come back to it later, open **Sessions** in the
Skill Track nav and pick it out of the list.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-page"
  caption="The session page. Everything else in this guide is one click from here."
/>

The **Contents** card lists what's in the session, with counts. Click a row to
change it:

- **Personnel**, **Skills** and **Assessors** open a dialog to choose them —
  steps 2 and 3 below.
- **Skill checks** opens a read-only log of every check recorded in the
  session, by every assessor, filterable by result and status.
- **Conflicts** only shows while two assessors' checks of the same person and
  skill are still unresolved. It opens the review page at its Conflicts card
  (step 5).

The page header carries **Record**, a menu holding the two recording views
(step 4), and the menu (⋮), which holds **History**, **Review**, **Edit** and
**Delete**, plus **Reopen** once the session is approved.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-record-menu"
  caption="Record opens the two recording views; the Contents card behind it reaches the session's other pages."
/>

## 2. Choose skills

Click **Skills** in the Contents card, then tick the skills this session covers.
They come from the packages your organisation subscribes to, grouped by package
and skill group. **Show skill descriptions** adds each skill's description, to
help you choose. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-skills"
  caption="Ticking the skills this session covers, under each package's skill groups."
/>

## 3. Choose personnel and assessors

Click **Personnel**, then tick who is being assessed, grouped by team, and
**Save**. Only active teams are listed, so someone who isn't on an active team
can't be added.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-personnel"
  caption="Who is being assessed, one section per team — the count in the team header tracks your ticks."
/>

**Assessors** works the same way, for who can record checks in the session.
Several assessors can record at once, each working through their own share. An
assessor needs the Skills Assessor or Skills Admin role, and a linked person
record.

## 4. Record results

This is the actual assessing, done from either **By Person** or **By Skill** —
the two entries in the header's **Record** menu. They are two views over the
same recording control, both laid out as a picker on the left and a scrollable
list of rows on the right:

- **By Person** — pick an assessee on the left; every skill in the session
  lists on the right.
- **By Skill** — pick a skill on the left; everyone in the session lists on the
  right.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-by-person"
  caption="By Person — an assessee picked on the left, the session's skills to record on the right."
/>

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-by-skill"
  caption="By Skill — the same screen with the axes swapped: a skill on the left, everyone in the session on the right."
/>

Each row has a **Fail** button, a **Pass** button and a **More** button (**⋯**).
Every tap saves straight away — there's no separate save step:

- **Fail** or **Pass** records that result for the row at once. The one that
  matches the row's current result is highlighted.
- Tapping the highlighted button again clears the check, and the confirmation
  that pops up offers **Undo** if you didn't mean to. If the check has a note,
  the tap opens the dialog below instead, so a stray tap can't throw the note
  away.
- Any other result — a different tier such as Strong Pass, or Not Taught —
  shows as its name in place of the Fail and Pass buttons.
- A small note icon appears before the buttons when the check has a note.

**More** opens a dialog for that person and skill, with the skill's description,
every result your organisation has enabled (see
[Skill Track](/docs/skill-track#skill-checks) for what each one means) and a
free-text **Notes** field. Pick a result, add a note if you like, then **Save**.
**Delete** removes the check (again with **Undo**), and **Cancel** closes the
dialog without changing anything.

While a tap is saving, its row dims briefly and its buttons wait until it's
done. Try it below — this is the same row and dialog you'll see in the app:

### Working with other assessors

You only see, and change, your own checks. When another assessor has checked
the same person and skill, the row says **Also checked by**, with their name
and result. It keeps up as they record, within about ten seconds, so you can see
who has already been covered.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-also-checked-by"
  caption="Another assessor's check on the same person and skill, shown under the skill's name."
/>

Two checks of the same person and skill are a **conflict**. Nothing stops you
recording one — the review settles which check counts (step 5).

### Recording options

**Options**, in the page header, opens **Recording Options**:

- **Recording Mode** — switch between **By Person** and **By Skill**.
- **Skill Order** — **By Package/Group** (the default) nests skills under
  their package and skill group, in catalogue order, with anything no longer
  part of an assessable group collected under "Other." **Alphabetical** lists
  skills by name instead.
- **Configure Session** — change the session's **Personnel**, **Skills** or
  **Assessors** without leaving the page.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-recording-options"
  caption="Recording Options: the recording view, the skill order, and the session's personnel, skills and assessors."
/>

## 5. Review and approve

**Review**, in the session's menu (⋮), is where a Skills Admin approves the
session. Anyone else who can see the session gets the same page, read-only.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-review"
  caption="The review page: the summary tiles, then the Conflicts card — six resolved, three still to go."
/>

The tiles at the top sum the session up:

- **Unique checks** — how many person and skill pairs have a check. Two
  assessors checking the same pair count once.
- **Personnel** and **Skills** — how many have at least one check.
- **Coverage** — the share of the session's person and skill pairs that have a
  check.

The menu (⋮) beside **Approve** has **Unassessed personnel** and **Unassessed
skills**. Untick one to leave the personnel or skills with no checks out of the
figures — useful when someone didn't turn up.

Below the tiles are three cards:

- **Conflicts** lists each person and skill with more than one check, and
  whether the results differ. **Resolve** shows the checks side by side: pick
  the one to include in the approval, or **Exclude all**, then **Save**. A
  resolved conflict shows what was picked, and **Change** picks again.
- **Personnel** and **Skills** list each one's checks and coverage. Click one to
  see its checks, untick any you want to leave out of the approval, then
  **Save**. Checks in a conflict can't be changed there; settle them with
  **Resolve**.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/session-resolve-conflict"
  caption="Resolving a conflict: pick which assessor's check counts, or exclude them all."
/>

Every check is included unless you exclude it. Each **Save** is kept straight
away, so you can leave a review half done and come back to it.

**Approve** stays disabled while the session has no checks, or until every
conflict is resolved. Clicking it asks you to confirm how many checks will be
approved and how many excluded. Approving keeps the included checks, which then
count in reports, and locks the session.

## Approved sessions

An approved session's personnel, skills, assessors, checks and date are locked.
Its name and notes stay editable. The recording pages say the session has been
approved, and the **Record** menu's views are disabled.

To change anything else, a Skills Admin **Reopens** it from the session's menu
(⋮). The session goes back to **Draft**, its included checks go back to
**Pending review**, and you can record, review and approve again. Until it's
approved again, its results leave the reports.

## Deleting a session

**Delete**, in the session's menu, is for Skills Admins. It's permanent — a
session doesn't go to the Rubbish bin.

The checks recorded in the session are kept. Approved ones still count in
reports, now as checks outside any session. The rest never will.

## Session status

A session is **Draft** while it's being worked on and **Approved** once it's
approved. Each of its checks has a status too, shown in the session's **Skill
checks** log:

| Status         | Meaning                                                           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft          | Recorded but not yet reviewed.                                    |
| Pending review | Included in an earlier approval, before the session was reopened. |
| Approved       | Included in the approval — its result counts in reports.          |
| Excluded       | Left out of the approval; its result doesn't count.               |
