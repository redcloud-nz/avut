# Reports

All four reports summarize the same underlying competency data, just sliced
differently. Each one opens a dialog to pick its scope (a person, a team, or a
skill) before showing results.

Every report uses the same four-state status for a person/skill pairing:

| Status        | Meaning                                                  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current       | Competent, and still within the skill's currency window. |
| Expired       | Was competent, but the currency window has lapsed.       |
| Not Competent | The most recent recorded result wasn't a pass.           |
| Not Assessed  | No check on record for this person/skill pairing.        |

## Personnel Competency

Shows one person's status across every skill your organization has
subscribed to. Pick the person from the scope dialog; use it to see one
person's full competency picture at a glance, or to prep for a
re-assessment by spotting what's expired or missing.

## Team Competency

Summarizes competency **per skill** across a group of people — pick a
specific team or the whole organization from the scope dialog. Rather than
listing every person, each skill shows how many people in scope are Current,
Expired, Not Competent, or Not Assessed, so it's the quickest way to spot a
skill your team is falling behind on.

## Personnel × Skill Matrix

A grid with every person in scope down one side and every skill across the
top, each cell colored by status. Pick a team or the whole organization from
the scope dialog — this is the report to reach for when you want the full
picture at once, rather than one person or one skill at a time.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/report-matrix"
  caption="Skills down the side, people across the top, one status icon per pairing."
/>

Two things in the grid are clickable. A **skill name** down the left opens
its description and how often it needs reassessing — useful when a row's
colour makes you ask what the skill actually demands.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/report-matrix-skill"
  caption="Clicking a skill name: what it covers, and its reassessment interval."
/>

A **cell** opens the check behind it: the result, when it was assessed and
when it expires (with both as plain dates and as "10 months ago"), who
assessed it, and any note they left. The popover shows the check, not the
pairing, so it doesn't repeat the person and skill you clicked.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/report-matrix-cell"
  caption="Clicking a cell: the recorded check, its dates, the assessor, and their note."
/>

## Skill Coverage

Flips the matrix around: pick one skill from the scope dialog (optionally
narrowed to a team) and see everyone's status for it. Use this when the
question is "who can do X" rather than "how is this person or team doing
overall."
