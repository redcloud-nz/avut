# Packages

A **skill package** is a bundle of related skills published by an organisation,
such as a first-aid package or an equipment-operation package. **Packages**
lists every published package, in two parts:

- **Subscribed** — the packages your organisation subscribes to.
- **Available** — the rest, which you can search.

Each group and each skill opens its own page. A group's page lists its skills.
A skill's page shows its details, which the package's author sets, such as its
**Frequency**: how often it should be reassessed. If you can view checks,
**Skill Coverage** under **Related** opens the
[report](/docs/skill-track/reports) for that skill.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/skill-page"
  caption="A skill's page, with Skill Coverage under Related for anyone who can view checks."
/>

Skill, group and package names link to these pages wherever they appear in
Skill Track.

A group or skill can carry two flags, set by the package's author:

- **Not included by default** — once enforced, this will decide whether the
  skill or group is available to your organisation at all.
- **Required** — once enforced, this will decide whether the skill counts
  towards coverage statistics in reports.

<Callout>
  Neither flag is enforced yet — they're descriptive only for now. Choosing which skills belong in a
  session is always a manual step on the session's **Skills** page (see
  [Sessions](/docs/skill-track/sessions)), and every subscribed skill counts in reports regardless
  of "Required". Turning these into organisation-customizable settings is planned for a future
  update.
</Callout>

## Skills

**Skills** lists every skill your organisation can assess, in one table: the
skills of each package you subscribe to, with their group, package and
frequency. It's the same set the session skill picker uses. Search by skill,
group or package name, or filter by **Package**.

<Screenshot
  id="skill-track/skills-list"

/>

An **Archived** skill is one its author has retired. It's still listed, but the
reports and the package's page leave it out.

## Subscribing

A package's skills only become assessable once your organisation
**subscribes** to it. Subscribing is an organisation-wide decision, not a
per-user one, and only a **Skills Admin** can make it, from the package's page:
**Subscribe** is a button, and **Unsubscribe** is in the page's menu (⋮). Each
asks you to confirm.

- **Subscribing** makes every skill in the package available to your sessions
  and reports.
- **Unsubscribing** takes the package's skills out of the session skill picker
  and the reports straight away. Checks already recorded against them are kept,
  and count again if you subscribe again.

Packages aren't versioned. When the author changes a package, subscribers see
the change straight away.

## Withdrawn packages

If a package's author unpublishes, archives or deletes it, it's **withdrawn**
from every subscriber. It stays under **Subscribed** in **Packages**, marked
**Withdrawn**, and its page shows **Subscribed · Withdrawn**. Its skills are out of your sessions and reports until the author publishes
it again. A session that already had its skills lists them under **No longer
available** when you change the session's skills. Checks already recorded are
kept, and you can still unsubscribe.

The same happens to a single group when its author deletes it: its skills drop
out of your sessions, reports and the **Skills** list.

Writing and publishing packages is a separate area, the
[Skill Package Builder](/docs/skill-package-builder), for the Skills Author
role.
