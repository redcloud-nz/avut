# Catalogue

The catalogue lists **skill packages** available to your organization — bundles
of related skills published by AVUT or by another organization, such as a
first-aid package or an equipment-operation package. Every organization can see
the packages on offer, whether or not they've subscribed yet.

A group or skill carries two flags in the catalogue view:

- **Included** — shown as "Not included by default" when off. Once enforced,
  this will decide whether the skill/group is available to your organization
  at all.
- **Required** — once enforced, this will decide whether the skill counts
  towards coverage statistics in reports.

<Callout>
  Neither flag is enforced yet — they're descriptive only for now. Choosing which skills belong in a
  session is always a manual step on the session's **Skills** page (see
  [Sessions](/docs/skill-track/sessions)), and every subscribed skill counts in reports regardless
  of "Required". Turning these into organization-customizable settings is planned for a future
  update.
</Callout>

## Subscribing

A package's skills only become assessable once your organization
**subscribes** to it — a one-click action (with a confirmation dialog) on the
package page. Subscribing is an organization-wide decision, not a per-user one:
once subscribed, the package's skills are available to every session your
organization runs. **Unsubscribe** the same way to remove it.

Building and publishing packages themselves is a separate area, available to
the `skill-package-author` role.
