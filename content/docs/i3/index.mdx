# I3 — Issue, Inspect, Return

The I3 module tracks individually issued items — personal protective
equipment and the like — issued to your members. It works on your D4H
equipment records: issuing an item creates it in D4H.

## Before you start

I3 needs:

- the module and the **D4H Integration** turned on in
  [Organisation Settings](/docs/admin/organization#integrations), and
- your own personal D4H access token, added under **User Settings → D4H**.

You see the D4H teams your token can see, and what you can do follows your D4H
account's permissions.

## What's here

- **By Equipment Type** and **By Member** show what's been issued to a team,
  read from D4H.
- **Issue Items** is where you issue equipment, using a form that keeps your
  drafts until you submit one.
- **Templates** are the items you can issue.

**Return Items** and inspections aren't built yet.

## Issuing items

Start an issue form and pick the member. Add each item from a template, with
its variant and serial number if it has them, add any comments, and submit the
form. Submitting
creates an equipment record in D4H for each item — as long as your D4H account
can create equipment for the member's team.

<Callout type="warning">
  If one item fails partway through, the records already created in D4H stay there; they aren't
  rolled back. Check D4H before submitting the form again.
</Callout>

## Templates

A **template** describes an item you can issue. Each one is linked to a D4H
equipment **category** and **kind**, so the records it creates land in the right
place, and can have variants such as sizes. Deleting a template moves it to the
[Rubbish bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin).

## Roles

- An **I3 Editor** can issue items.
- An **I3 Admin** can also create, change and delete templates.
