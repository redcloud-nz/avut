# I3 — Issue, Inspect, Return

The I3 module tracks equipment through its lifecycle with your people: **issued**
to someone, **inspected** on a schedule, and **returned** when no longer needed.
It also holds the PPE templates that describe what a kit should contain.

## D4H

If your organization connects D4H, templates can be linked to D4H equipment so
issued items stay in step with the D4H inventory.
