There are two sets of notes. **Notes** in your organisation's sidebar are
shared with its members. **Notes** in your own account are personal: only you
can see them.

- **New note** creates an **Untitled note** and opens it. Sort the list by
  **Last updated**, **Created** or **Title**.
- **Edit** makes the title and body editable, with a formatting toolbar.
  Nothing is saved until you click **Save**; **Cancel** drops your changes.
- The menu (⋮) has **History**, which lists every change, and **Delete**.
  Deleting is permanent: a note doesn't go to the Rubbish bin.

In an organisation, every member can read notes and add their own. You can edit
or delete the notes you wrote; owners and admins can edit or delete anyone's.
