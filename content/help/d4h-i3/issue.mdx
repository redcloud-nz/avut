**Issue** lists your draft forms, newest first. Only you see them. **New** starts
one and opens it. A draft saves itself as you fill it in, and the form shows
**Saved** once it has. The bin button at the bottom discards it.

Pick the member under **Issued to** first, typing to search by name or team.
You're recorded under **Recorded by**. **Add Item** opens once you've picked
someone, and only offers templates whose D4H records belong to the member's team
or your D4H organisation. If you change the member, an item that no longer fits
is marked **Can't be issued to** that team.

**Submit** stays disabled while an item doesn't fit, or if your D4H account can't
create equipment in the member's team. Submitting creates a D4H equipment record
for each item, held by the member, and emails them a receipt and the
notification lists a summary.

If D4H refuses some items, the rest are still created and aren't undone. Check
the member's equipment in D4H before issuing the missing ones again.

You need the **I3 Editor** or **I3 Admin** role.
