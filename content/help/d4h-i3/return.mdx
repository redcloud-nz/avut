**Return** has **Drafts** and **Submitted** tabs. **Drafts** lists your draft
forms, newest first. Only you see them. **New** starts one and opens it. A draft
saves itself as you fill it in, and the bin button at the bottom discards it.
**Submitted** lists the forms you've submitted, newest first. Opening one shows
it read-only, with the D4H tasks it queued and where each stands. An item no
task was queued for is marked **Not returned**.

Pick the member handing items back under **Returned by**, typing to search by
name or team. You're recorded under **Recorded by**. Their items are listed with
each one's ref, type, brand, model and serial number: tick each one they're
handing back. Items already returned and waiting to be retired in D4H aren't
listed, and picking a different member clears the ticks.

**Submit** doesn't change D4H by itself. It queues a D4H task to retire each
item, for someone to finish under **D4H** › **Tasks**, and emails the
notification lists and the member. An item that isn't the member's any more, or
is already retired or queued, is skipped, and the message after you submit says
why.

To return one item without a form, choose **Return** from the **Actions** menu on
its D4H item page.

You need the **I3 Editor** or **I3 Admin** role, and your D4H account must be
able to update equipment in the member's team.
