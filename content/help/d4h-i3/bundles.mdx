A **bundle** is a list of templates that go together, such as the kit a new
member needs. Open one and use the **+** button to build it.

**Compare with a team** opens a team picker, then a matrix of that team's members
against the bundle's templates. A template is left out if it's deleted, has no
D4H type, or isn't used by the team. Only items the team owns count.

**Held** means the member has any item of the template's D4H type, in any size.
Lost items don't count (the member page shows them). Anything else is
**missing**. Open a member to see their items against the bundle.

A held item can still be flagged in amber. **Incomplete** means a part the
template requires is blank: brand, model or serial number. **Doesn't match**
means its brand and model aren't one of the template's variants, or are set on
a template whose type has no brand or model. Both still count as held.

A bundle doesn't know who it applies to, so you decide whether a gap matters.

An **I3 Admin** manages bundles. An **I3 Editor** can view and compare them.
