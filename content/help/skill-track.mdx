Skill Track records who has been assessed on which skills, and when.

- **Catalogue** — the skill packages your organisation has subscribed to, and
  what they contain.
- **Sessions** — the usual way to run assessments: pick who and what, record
  results, then review and approve.
- **Checks** — recording a single check on the spot, outside any session.
- **Reports** — competency and currency, by person, by team, by skill, or as
  one big matrix.

A **skill check** is a single record that someone was assessed on a skill, with
a result and a date. Only the three pass tiers (Weak Pass, Pass, Strong Pass)
count as competent.
