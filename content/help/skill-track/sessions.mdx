A **skill check session** is the usual way to run assessments — for example,
everything assessed on one training day. A session moves through five steps:
create it, choose skills, choose personnel, record results, then review and approve.

Start from the Sessions list with **New Session**. Name and date are both
optional: leave the name blank to take the next session number, and the date to
take today.

On the session's own page, the **Contents** card lists **Personnel**,
**Skills** and **Skill checks** with their counts; click a row to open it. The
page header carries **Record**, a menu holding the two recording views, and
**Review**.
