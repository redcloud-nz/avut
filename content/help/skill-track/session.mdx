A session's page shows its details, including its team if it has one, and a
tile for each part of the session. Click one to change it:

- **Personnel**: who is assessed. You pick them by team, so someone on no
  active team can't be added.
- **Skills**: what they're assessed on, from the packages your organisation
  subscribes to.
- **Assessors**: who can record checks. An assessor needs the Skills Assessor
  or Skills Admin role and a linked person record.
- **Skill checks**: a read-only log of every check recorded, by every assessor.
- **Conflicts**: only shown while two assessors' checks of the same person and
  skill are still unresolved. Resolve them on the review page.

Opened from a team, another team's session (or one with no team) is read-only:
a notice says so, and nothing on it can be changed from there, so only
**Review** is shown beside the details. Open it from the organisation to change
it.

Otherwise, beside the details, **Record** opens the two recording views (by
person or by skill), and **Review** opens the review page. Once the session is
approved, Record offers **Reopen** instead. The pencil on the Session card edits
its name, time, team and notes; **Delete** is in the card's menu (⋮), and
**Updated**, at its foot, opens the session's history.

Approving locks everything about the session (its name, notes, time, personnel,
skills, assessors and checks) until a Skills Admin reopens it.

**Delete** is for Skills Admins and can't be undone. The checks recorded in the
session are kept: approved ones still count in reports, and the rest never
will.
