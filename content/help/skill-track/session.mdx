A session's page shows its details and a **Contents** card. Click a row to
change it:

- **Personnel**: who is assessed. You pick them by team, so someone on no
  active team can't be added.
- **Skills**: what they're assessed on, from the packages your organisation
  subscribes to.
- **Assessors**: who can record checks. An assessor needs the Skills Assessor
  or Skills Admin role and a linked person record.
- **Skill checks**: a read-only log of every check recorded, by every assessor.
- **Conflicts**: only shown when two assessors checked the same person and
  skill. Resolve them on the review page.

**Record** opens the two recording views. The menu (⋮) holds **History**,
**Review**, **Edit**, **Reopen** and **Delete**.

Once a session is approved, it's locked: nothing can be recorded or changed,
and its date can't be edited, until a Skills Admin reopens it.

**Delete** is for Skills Admins and can't be undone. The checks recorded in the
session are kept: approved ones still count in reports, and the rest never
will.
