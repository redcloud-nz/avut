The catalogue lists the **skill packages** available to your organisation —
bundles of related skills published by AVUT or by another organisation, such as
a first-aid package. Every organisation can see the packages on offer, whether
or not it has subscribed yet.

Open a package to see its contents, organised into **skill groups**, each
holding a set of individual **skills**.
