**Catalogue** lists every published skill package, from any organisation, with
its publisher, how many skills it holds and how many organisations subscribe.
The ones your organisation subscribes to say **Subscribed**.

Open a package to see its skill groups and skills. **Required** and **Not
included by default** are set by the package's author; they don't change
anything yet.

Only a **Skills Admin** can **Subscribe** or **Unsubscribe**, from the
package's page:

- Subscribing makes every skill in the package available to your sessions.
  Reports show the active skills, while the package stays published and
  isn't archived by its author.
- Unsubscribing takes the package's skills out of the session skill picker and
  the reports straight away. Checks already recorded against them are kept, and
  count again if you subscribe again.

Packages aren't versioned. When the author changes a package, subscribers see
the change straight away.
