**Packages** lists every published skill package, from any organisation. The
ones your organisation subscribes to say **Subscribed**. If the author
withdraws or deletes a package you subscribe to, it's marked **Withdrawn**: its
skills are out of your sessions and reports until it's published again, and you
can still unsubscribe.

Open a package to see its skill groups and skills. A group opens its own page,
listing its skills, and each skill opens its page. **Required** and **Not
included by default** are set by the package's author; they don't change
anything yet.

Only a **Skills Admin** can **Subscribe** or **Unsubscribe**, from the
package's page:

- Subscribing makes the package's skills available to your sessions and
  reports, while it stays published and isn't archived by its author.
- Unsubscribing takes them out straight away. Checks already recorded are
  kept, and count again if you subscribe again.

Packages aren't versioned: an author's changes reach subscribers straight away.
