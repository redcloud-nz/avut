**Skills** lists every skill your organisation can assess: the skills of each
package you subscribe to, with their group and package. It's the same set the
session skill picker uses. A package its author has withdrawn drops out until
it's published again, and a skill or group its author has archived or deleted
drops out too. Checks already recorded for it stay in each person's history.

**Frequency** is how often the skill should be reassessed. A check older than
that counts as expired in the reports. **None** means a check never expires.

Search by skill, group or package name, or filter by **Package**.

Open a skill to see its details, which the package's author sets.
**Required** and **Included** don't change anything yet. An
**Archived** badge means the author has retired the skill or its group, so it's
out of your sessions and reports; a note at the top means they've withdrawn its
package.

If you can view checks, **Related** links to more about the skill. **Recent
checks** shows for every skill, archived ones included, and opens **Checks**
filtered to that skill. **Skill Coverage** opens the report for that skill. It
shows only for a skill you can assess: an active skill, in an active group, in a
package you subscribe to that hasn't been withdrawn.

To add skills, subscribe to another package in **Packages**.
