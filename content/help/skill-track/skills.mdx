**Skills** lists every skill your organisation can assess: the skills of each
package you subscribe to, with their group and package. It's the same set the
session skill picker uses. A package its author has withdrawn drops out until
it's published again, and a group its author has deleted drops out with its
skills. An **Archived** skill is still listed, but the reports
and the package's page leave it out.

**Frequency** is how often the skill should be reassessed. A check older than
that counts as expired in the reports. **None** means a check never expires.

Search by skill, group or package name, or filter by **Package**.

Open a skill to see its details, which the package's author sets.
**Required** and **Included** don't change anything yet. An
**Archived** badge means the author has retired the skill or its group; a note
at the top means they've withdrawn its package. If you can view checks,
**Skill Coverage** under **Related** opens the report for that skill. It shows only for an active
skill in a package you subscribe to that hasn't been withdrawn (an archived
group doesn't hide it).

To add skills, subscribe to another package in the **Catalogue**.
