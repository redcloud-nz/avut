**Skills** lists every skill your organisation can assess: the skills of each
package you subscribe to, with their group and package. A package its author
has withdrawn drops out until it's published again. An **Archived** skill is
still listed, but the reports and the package's page leave it out.

**Frequency** is how often the skill should be reassessed. **None** means a
check never expires.

Open a skill to see its details, which the package's author sets. An
**Archived** badge means the author has retired it; a note at the top means
they've withdrawn its package. If you can view checks, **Skill Coverage**
opens the report for that skill. It's absent for an archived skill.

To add skills, subscribe to another package in the **Catalogue**.
