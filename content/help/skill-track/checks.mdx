**Skill Checks** lists every check recorded in your organisation, newest first,
from sessions and from outside them, with its result, session, date and
assessor. A session's checks show here whether or not the session has been
approved.

Narrow the list with the filters above it: the person checked, the skill, the
session (or **No session** for checks added outside one), the assessor, the
result, and a range of dates. **Clear filters** shows every check again. The
filters are part of the page's address, so you can bookmark a filtered view or
share its link with someone else.

**Add Check** records a single check outside any session, for example an
informal observation or a result from before you used AVUT. Pick the person,
the skill and a result, and add notes if you like. **Date checked** is today
unless you pick an earlier day; reports date the check, and work out its expiry,
from that day. You're always recorded as the assessor, so you need a person
record linked to your account.

A check added here belongs to no session, so there's no review or approval: it
counts in the reports as soon as you add it.

Skills Assessors and Skills Admins can add checks. Skills Reporters can view
the list.
