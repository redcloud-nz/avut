**Recent Skill Checks** lists every check dated in the last month, from
sessions and from outside them, with its result, session, date and assessor.
A session's checks show here whether or not the session has been approved.

**Add Check** records a single check outside any session, for example an
informal observation. Pick the person, the skill and a result, and add notes if
you like. You're always recorded as the assessor, so you need a person record
linked to your account. The check is dated today; you can't back-date it.

A check added here belongs to no session, so it's never reviewed or approved,
and it doesn't count in the reports. To put a result on someone's record,
record it in a session and approve the session.

Skills Assessors and Skills Admins can add checks. Skills Reporters can view
the list.
