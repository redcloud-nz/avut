The **Checks** page is an organisation-wide log of skill checks recorded in the
last month, separate from any one session. It's for recording an **ad-hoc**
check — an informal or historical observation you want on record without
setting up a whole session.

To record one, pick a person, a skill and a result, and add notes if you want.
You're always the assessor for a check you record here.

Ad-hoc checks are recorded immediately. There's no review or approval step,
unlike checks recorded inside a session.
