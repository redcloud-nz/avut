Skill Track records who has been assessed on which skills, and when. Each link
opens one area. You only see the links your roles allow.

- **Assessors**: choose which members hold the **Skills Assessor** role.
- **Skill Catalogue**: the skill packages on offer, and the ones your
  organisation subscribes to.
- **Skill Checks**: checks from the last month, and adding one outside a
  session.
- **Skill Check Sessions**: the usual way to assess. Choose personnel and
  skills, record results, then review and approve.
- **Reports**: competency and currency by person, team or skill.

The module has its own roles. A **Skills Assessor** runs sessions and records
checks. A **Skills Admin** can also approve, reopen and delete sessions, and
subscribe to packages. A **Skills Reporter** can view checks, sessions and
reports, but not change them.

Only the three pass results count as competent, and only checks in an approved
session, or added on the **Checks** page, reach the reports.
