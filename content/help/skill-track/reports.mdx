**Skills Reports** has four reports over the same data:

- **Personnel Competency**: one person against every skill.
- **Team Competency**: for each skill, how a team or the whole organisation
  stands.
- **Personnel × Skill Matrix**: every person against every skill.
- **Skill Coverage**: everyone's status for one skill.

Each person gets one status per skill, from their most recent check in an
approved session:

- **Current**: a pass, within the skill's reassessment interval.
- **Expired**: a pass, but the interval has run out.
- **Not Competent**: any other result, including Exempt and Not Taught.
- **Not Assessed**: no approved check.

Only active personnel, and skills from the packages your organisation
subscribes to, appear. Checks in sessions that aren't approved, and checks
added on the **Checks** page, don't count.
