Pick a team, or **Whole Organisation**, to see one row per skill, grouped by
package and skill group. The bar splits the team's active members by status:
**Current**, **Expired**, **Not Competent** and **Not Assessed**. The figure
beside it is how many are current.

Use it to spot a skill the team is falling behind on. To see who, open the
**Personnel × Skill Matrix** or **Skill Coverage** for the same team.

**Change scope** picks another team. The menu (⋮) can show **Only Gaps** (skills
where not everyone is current) and each skill's description. Click a skill's
name for its description and reassessment interval.

Only checks in approved sessions count.
