title: Recording checks
description: Recording a session's results by person or by skill.

Pick a person (**Assess by Person**) or a skill (**Assess by Skill**) on the
left. The rows on the right are the session's skills, or its personnel. On a
phone, the picker is a dropdown.

Each row has a fail button, a pass button and **More** (⋯). Every tap saves
straight away:

- Tap fail or pass to record it. Tap the highlighted one again to clear the
  check; the message that pops up offers **Undo**. If the check has notes, the
  tap opens the dialog instead, so the notes aren't lost.
- Any other result, such as Not Taught, shows its name in place of the two
  buttons.
- **More** opens a dialog with the skill's description, every result your
  organisation has enabled, and **Notes**. **Delete** removes the check.

You only see and change your own checks. When another assessor has checked the
same person and skill, the row says **Also checked by** with their name and
result. The review page settles which check counts.

**Options** opens **Recording Options**: switch between **By Person** and **By
Skill**, change the **Skill Order**, or change the session's personnel, skills
or assessors.

To record, you need a linked person record and to be one of the session's
assessors. Once the session is approved, these pages are locked until it's
reopened.
