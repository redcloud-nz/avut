I3 tracks equipment issued to your members, using your D4H equipment records.
It needs the D4H integration turned on for your organisation, and your own
personal D4H access token, added in your account settings under **D4H**. You
see the D4H teams that token can see.

- **By Equipment Type** and **By Member** show what's been issued to a team,
  from D4H.
- **Issue Items** keeps your draft forms until you submit one. Submitting
  creates an equipment record in D4H for each item, if your D4H account can
  create equipment for the recipient's team. Records already created aren't
  undone if a later item fails.
- **Templates** are the items you can issue, each linked to a D4H equipment
  category and kind, with variants such as sizes. Deleting one moves it to the
  Rubbish bin.

**Return Items** and inspections aren't built yet.

An **I3 Editor** can issue items. An **I3 Admin** can also create, change and
delete templates.
