---
title: I3 — Issue, Inspect, Return
description: Tracking equipment as it is issued, inspected and returned.
guide: i3
keyTerms: ["ppe-template", "d4h"]
---

I3 tracks equipment through its life with your personnel: **issued** to
someone, **inspected** on a schedule, and **returned** when no longer needed. It
also holds the PPE templates that describe what a kit should contain.

If your organisation connects D4H, templates can be linked to D4H equipment so
issued items stay in step with the D4H inventory.
