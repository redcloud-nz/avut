---
title: D4H Views
description: Read-only views of your connected D4H data.
guide: d4h-views
keyTerms: ["d4h"]
---

D4H Views shows data from your organisation's **D4H** account inside AVUT,
read-only, so you can look up D4H members, equipment and activity without
switching tools. Nothing here changes D4H; edits still happen in D4H itself.

It only works when your organisation has configured a D4H access token. If you
don't see any data, ask an admin to check the D4H connection in settings.
