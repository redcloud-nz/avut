---
title: D4H Views
description: Read-only views of your D4H data.
guide: d4h-views
keyTerms: ["d4h"]
---

D4H Views shows your D4H data inside AVUT, read-only. Nothing here changes D4H;
make changes in D4H itself.

- **Equipment**: browse by **Equipment Brands** and their models, or by
  **Equipment Categories** and their kinds, down to individual items.
- **Members**: the D4H members of your teams.

It uses your own personal D4H access token, not one shared by the organisation,
so you see what your D4H account can see. Add a token in your account settings
under **D4H**.

The organisation also needs the D4H integration turned on in **Organisation
Settings**, where an owner or admin turns modules on and off too.
