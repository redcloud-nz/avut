D4H I3 tracks equipment issued to your members, using your D4H equipment records.
It needs the D4H integration and the D4H module turned on for your organisation, and your own
personal D4H access token, added in your account settings under **D4H**. You
see the D4H teams that token can see.

- **Issue Items** keeps your draft forms until you submit one. Submitting
  creates an equipment record in D4H for each item. If your D4H account can't
  create equipment for the recipient's team, a warning shows and **Submit** is
  disabled. Records already created aren't
  undone if a later item fails.
- **Return Items** records equipment coming back from a person. Pick who is
  returning, tick the items they hand in and add any comments. Submitting queues a D4H task
  for each item, noting who received it, if your D4H account can update equipment
  in their team. Nothing changes in D4H until someone applies the task under
  **D4H** › **Tasks**.
- **Return** is also in the **Actions** menu of a D4H item page, for an item
  issued to a member.
- **Templates** are the items you can issue, each linked to a D4H equipment
  category and kind, with variants such as sizes. Deleting one moves it to the
  Rubbish bin.

Inspections aren't built yet.

An **I3 Editor** can issue and return items. An **I3 Admin** can also create,
change and delete templates.
