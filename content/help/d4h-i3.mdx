D4H I3 tracks equipment issued to your members, using your D4H equipment records.
It needs the D4H integration and module turned on, and your D4H access token
under account settings › **D4H**.

- **Issue Items** keeps your drafts until you submit. Pick the recipient first:
  **Add Item** needs one, and only templates whose D4H records belong to the
  recipient's team or its organisation are offered. An item that doesn't fit, or
  a D4H account that can't create equipment for that team, disables **Submit**.
  Records already created aren't undone if a later item fails.
- **Return Items** queues a D4H task for each ticked item. Nothing changes in D4H
  until someone applies it under **D4H** › **Tasks**. **Return** is also in the
  **Actions** menu of an issued D4H item.
- **Notifications**: Issue and Return email everyone on the organisation-wide
  list and the recipient team's list, set under organisation settings ›
  **Modules** › **D4H I3**. The member also gets a receipt at their D4H email.
- **Issued Types** shows how many of each type one D4H team has out. Open a type
  to see who holds which items.
- **Templates** are the items you can issue, each linked to a D4H category and
  type, with variants such as sizes. Pickers group records by owner. On a
  template's page, hover a category, type, brand or model to see its owner. Turn on
  **Organisation-owned records only** under organisation settings › **Modules** ›
  **D4H I3** to offer only your linked D4H organisation's records; other templates
  get a **Not organisation records** badge. Deleting a template moves it to the Rubbish bin.

An **I3 Editor** can issue and return items; an **I3 Admin** can also change templates.
