D4H Attendance Reminders emails a team's members whose attendance on an
upcoming D4H exercise is still **Requested**, asking them to answer in D4H.

- **Rules** say when to remind each team: a **Day** and **Time** each week, in
  your organisation's time zone, and how many hours to **Look ahead** for
  exercises. Add one with **New Rule**. A rule that can't run, for example
  because its team isn't linked to D4H, says why in its **Status**.
- A rule's page lists its **runs**. **Run Now** sends straight away, and **Dry
  Run** shows who would be reminded without sending anything. A run lists each
  member and its **Outcome**. No one is reminded about the same exercise twice.
- **Opt-outs** lists members who've stopped their reminders, from the link in
  every email, or because an admin added them with **Add Opt-out**.

The reminders read D4H with the organisation token chosen under organisation
settings › **Modules** › **D4H Attendance Reminders**. Replies go to the
organisation's **Reply-To address**, under **General**.

Only owners and admins can see and change the reminders.
