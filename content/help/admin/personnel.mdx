**Personnel** has a person record for everyone your organisation tracks. A
person doesn't need a login; link them to a user account only if they'll sign
in.

The list shows **Active** personnel; change the **Status** filter to see
archived ones. Owners and admins add someone with **New Person** (a name and an
email).

Open a person to see their details, their teams and any linked user account.
Owners and admins can:

- **Edit** their name or email with the pencil on the Person card.
- **Add to team** from the Team Memberships list.
- **Invite to AVUT**: email them an invitation. Accepting it links their new
  account to this record. If the email already belongs to a member, it offers to
  link that account instead.
- **Link to existing user**: connect an existing member's account. **Unlink
  from user**, in the Linked User card's menu (⋮), disconnects it. Neither
  record is deleted.
- **Archive**, from the Person card's menu (⋮): keep their teams and history,
  but drop them from the Active list. **Restore** brings them back.
- **Delete**, from the same menu: move the record to the Rubbish bin, where it
  can be recovered until it's purged. Their skill checks stay until the record
  is purged; checks they assessed others on are kept after that.

Invite and Link are buttons beside the record while the person is Active and
has no user yet; for an archived person, **Link to user** is in the menu.

**Updated**, at the foot of the Person card, opens the record's history: every
change made to it and who made it.
