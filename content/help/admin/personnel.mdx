**Personnel** has a person record for everyone your organisation tracks. A
person doesn't need a login; link them to a user account only if they'll sign
in.

The list shows **Active** personnel; change the **Status** filter to see
archived ones. Owners and admins add someone with **New Person** (a name and an
email).

Open a person to see their details, their teams and any linked user account.
Owners and admins can use the menu on their page to:

- **Edit** their name or email.
- **Add to team**.
- **Invite to AVUT**: email them an invitation. Accepting it links their new
  account to this record. If the email already belongs to a member, it offers to
  link that account instead.
- **Link to user** or **Unlink from user**: connect an existing member's
  account, or disconnect it. Neither record is deleted.
- **Archive**: keep their teams and history, but drop them from the Active
  list. **Restore** brings them back.
- **Delete**: move the record to the Rubbish bin, where it can be recovered
  until it's purged. Their skill checks stay until the record is purged; checks
  they assessed others on are kept after that.

**History**, in the same menu, lists every change made to the record and who
made it.
