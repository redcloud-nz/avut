**Organisation** shows your organisation's name, slug and ID. Owners and admins
change the name and slug with **Edit**. The slug is the part of the address
after `/orgs/`, so changing it changes every link to your organisation.

Once a team has been linked to D4H, owners and admins also see a **D4H
Integration** card with the D4H organisation, its server and when it was last
synced. While a team is linked, **Sync with D4H** refreshes those details.
**Unlink from D4H** only works once no team is linked.

Everyone can view **Organisation Settings**; only owners and admins can change
them.

- **Personnel**: whether AVUT links a person record to a member's account by
  matching email, when an invitation is accepted or when a person is added.
- **Rubbish Bin**: how many days deleted records are kept.
- **Integrations**: D4H, with its default server, and email.
- **Modules**: turn each module on or off. Disabling one hides it for everyone,
  but nothing is deleted. The Skill Track card also sets which check results you
  can record and what they're called.
