**Invitations** lists every invitation sent from your organisation, newest
first, with the roles it grants and its status: **Pending**, **Accepted**,
**Rejected** or **Cancelled**. Use the **Roles** and **Status** filters to
narrow the list.

Owners and admins send one with **New Invitation**: enter the email address and
tick the roles they'll get. Roles for a module only appear while that module is
enabled. The person is emailed a link to sign in, or sign up, and join.

A pending invitation's menu has **Resend**, which sends it again, and
**Revoke**, which cancels it.

If the person is already in **Personnel**, invite them with **Invite to AVUT**
on their person record instead: accepting that invitation links their account to
the record. An invitation sent from this page links them only if **Link on
invitation accept** is on in **Organisation Settings** and a person record has the
same email.
