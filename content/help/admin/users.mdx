**Users** lists the members of your organisation: the accounts that can sign in
here, the person record each one is linked to, and their roles. You can't add a
user on this page. Invite them from **Invitations**, or from their person record
in **Personnel**.

Open a user to see their details. Owners and admins can:

- **Edit** their roles with the pencil on the User card. Roles combine freely. A
  role that another role already includes shows ticked and greyed out, and a
  module's roles only appear while that module is enabled.
- **Link person**, the button beside the record while they have no person
  record, or **Unlink person**, in the Linked Person card's menu (⋮). Some
  screens, such as recording a skill check yourself, need you to have a linked
  person.
- **Delete** them, from the User card's menu (⋮), which removes them from this
  organisation straight away. It doesn't go to the Rubbish bin, and their
  sign-in account and person record are kept. You can't delete yourself.

A user's **Teams** card lists the teams they have access to. It is read-only:
grant or revoke team access from each team's page.

**Make owner** and **Remove owner**, in the User card's menu, grant and take
away ownership. Only an owner sees them, and you can't remove your own
ownership. Owners can do everything
an admin can, plus manage other owners. Only an owner can delete another owner.
