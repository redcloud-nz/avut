**Users** lists the members of your organisation: the accounts that can sign in
here, the person record each one is linked to, and their roles. You can't add a
user on this page. Invite them from **Invitations**, or from their person record
in **Personnel**.

Open a user to see their details. Owners and admins can:

- **Edit** their roles. Roles combine freely. A role that another role already
  includes shows ticked and greyed out, and a module's roles only appear while
  that module is enabled.
- **Link person** or **Unlink** their person record. Some screens, such as
  recording a skill check yourself, need you to have a linked person.
- **Delete** them, which removes them from this organisation straight away. It
  doesn't go to the Rubbish bin, and their sign-in account and person record are
  kept. You can't delete yourself.

**Make owner** and **Remove owner** grant and take away ownership. Only an owner
can use them, and you can't remove your own ownership. Owners can do everything
an admin can, plus manage other owners. Only an owner can delete another owner.
