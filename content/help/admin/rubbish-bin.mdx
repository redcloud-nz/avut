---
title: Rubbish
description: Recovering deleted records, or deleting them for good.
guide: admin#whats-here
---

Deleting a record in AVUT doesn't remove it straight away; it moves here.
**Rubbish** lists deleted personnel, teams, team memberships, I3 templates and
skill packages, groups and skills, most recent first. You only see the kinds of
record you're allowed to delete.

Each row has two buttons:

- **Recover** puts the record back.
- **Delete forever** removes it and everything that belongs to it, once you
  confirm. This can't be undone.

The **Purges** column shows when a record will be removed automatically. Deleted
records are kept for 30 days unless your organisation has changed that under
**Rubbish Bin** in **Organisation Settings** (1 to 90 days). **Not scheduled** means
the deletion date isn't known, so the record is never purged automatically.

A record can be held back from being deleted for good, automatically or with
**Delete forever**, for example a skill that another organisation has recorded
checks against. It stays here until that changes.
