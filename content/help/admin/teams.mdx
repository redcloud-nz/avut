**Teams** groups your personnel. Other modules use them too, for example to
report on a team's skills. The list shows **Active** teams; owners and admins
add one with **New Team**.

A team's **Related** card shows how many personnel are on it. Open it for the
member list, where **New Member** adds someone. Open a member to see the
membership, and use **Remove from team** to take them off; the membership goes
to the Rubbish bin.

The team's menu has **Edit**, **Add person**, **Archive** and **Delete**.
Archiving keeps the members and history, but the team takes no new members and
stops syncing with D4H; **Restore** undoes it. Deleting moves the team to the
Rubbish bin. **History** lists every change.

**D4H.** With the D4H integration on, owners and admins can **Link to D4H** an
active team. Pick a D4H team your personal access token can see, and its members
are imported. **Sync with D4H** previews the changes before you apply them.
Members from D4H carry a **D4H** badge. On a linked team, people you add by hand
carry **Manual**, and syncing leaves them alone. **Unlink from D4H** stops the
syncing but keeps the personnel and memberships.
