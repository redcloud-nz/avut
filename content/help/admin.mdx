The Admin module is where an organisation's **owners** and **admins** manage who
has access and how the organisation is set up. Every organisation has it.

- **Personnel** — the person records for your organisation. A person record can
  exist without a login attached.
- **Users** — accounts that can sign in, and the role each one holds.
- **Teams** — groupings of personnel used across other modules.
- **Invitations** — outstanding invites to join the organisation.
- **Organisation Settings** — which modules and integrations are enabled, how
  long deleted records stay in the Rubbish bin, and how person records link to
  user accounts. Everyone can see these settings; owners and admins can change
  them.

Roles decide what someone can do: `owner` and `admin` manage the organisation,
`member` has everyday access, and the other roles grant extra rights in
specific modules.
