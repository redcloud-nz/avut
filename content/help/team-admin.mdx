**Team Admin** looks after this team from its own space, without going
through the organisation's **Admin**.

The **Team** card shows the team's name, slug and description. Change them with
**Edit**, from the card's pencil or the menu. You can change the **Slug** here,
which moves the team's space to the new address, but not clear it: a team needs
a slug to have its own space.

**Access** lists the users who can use this space. Grant access with **+** and
revoke it from a user's row. Revoking your own access takes you out of the
space.

With the D4H integration on, link the team to a D4H team, sync its members, or
unlink it from the menu. Once it's linked, **Linked D4H Team** shows the D4H team
it follows, with a sync button.

**Related** shows how many personnel are on the team; open it for the member
list. There, **New Member** adds someone to the team, and the remove button on
a row takes them off.

Archiving or deleting the team, and clearing its slug, stay in the
organisation's **Admin → Teams**.
