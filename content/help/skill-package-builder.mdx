The **Skill Package Builder** is where your organisation writes skill packages
for any organisation to use from its Skill Track catalogue. You need the
**Skills Author** role.

**New** starts a package. **Import** loads a package exported as `.json`: a
package already here is updated in place, anything the file leaves out is
archived, and the result is always unpublished. Open a package to add its
groups and skills. A skill's **Revalidation Frequency** is in months and
decides when a pass expires in reports; 0 means it never expires. Deleting a
group takes its skills out of subscribers' sessions and reports with it, until
you recover the group from the Rubbish bin.

The package's menu (⋮) has:

- **History**, which lists every change to the package, including its groups
  and skills. Groups and skills have their own History in their menus.
- **Publish**, which puts it in every organisation's catalogue, and
  **Unpublish**, which takes it out. Unpublishing withdraws its skills from
  subscribers' sessions and reports until you publish it again; the checks they
  recorded are kept.
- **Archive** and **Restore**. Archiving also unpublishes, so it withdraws the
  skills the same way. Restoring doesn't publish again: do that yourself.
- **Delete**, which unpublishes the package and moves it to the Rubbish bin.
  Subscribers lose its skills straight away, but still see it as withdrawn, so
  they can unsubscribe.
- **Export .json**, to copy it to another organisation or AVUT instance.

Packages aren't versioned: a change to a published package reaches every
subscriber straight away.
