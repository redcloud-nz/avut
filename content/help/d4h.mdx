D4H shows your D4H teams, members and equipment inside AVUT.

- **Teams** lists the D4H teams you can see. A team's page links to its
  **Members**.
- **Members** asks you to pick a team, then lists that team's members. Use
  **Change team** to switch. A member's page shows the equipment issued to them.
- **Equipment Categories** and **Equipment Brands** cover every team, with an
  **Owner** column for the team or organisation each belongs to. A category's
  page lists its types, and a type's page lists its items in every team, with
  who holds each one. A brand's page lists its models, and a model's page lists
  its items in every team.
- An **item page** shows one piece of equipment, with its **Parents** and
  **Contents** and any open **D4H Tasks**. The pencil on its **D4H Item** card
  changes the item in D4H. When you can't edit it, hover the pencil to see why.
- **Open in D4H** on a team, member, category, type, brand, model or item page
  opens the same record in D4H, in a new tab.
- **Tasks** lists changes D4H can't take automatically: retiring an item,
  setting its serial or its brand and model, or marking it lost. Make the
  change in D4H, then press **Check**, which looks at the item in D4H and closes
  the task once the change is there, or **Dismiss** the task.

Editing items and **Tasks** need an I3 role, or the Admin or Owner role.
Editing also needs your own D4H account to be able to update equipment in the
item's team. You see a team's retire tasks if your D4H account can retire its
equipment, and its other tasks if it can update its equipment.

It uses your own personal D4H access token, not one shared by the organisation,
so you see what your D4H account can see. Add a token in your account settings
under **D4H**.

The organisation also needs the D4H integration turned on in **Organisation
Settings**, where an owner or admin turns modules on and off too.
