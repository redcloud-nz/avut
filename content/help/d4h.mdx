D4H shows your D4H teams, members and equipment inside AVUT.

- **Teams** lists the D4H teams you can see. A team's page links to its
  **Members**.
- **Members** asks you to pick a team, then lists that team's members. Use
  **Change team** to switch. A member's page shows the equipment issued to them.
- **Categories**, **Types** and **Brands** cover every team, with an **Owner**
  column for the team each belongs to. A type's page lists its items in every
  team, with who holds each one.
- An **item page** shows one piece of equipment, with its **Parents** and
  **Contents**, any open **D4H Tasks**, and an **Open in D4H** link. **Edit** in
  the **Actions** menu changes the item in D4H.
- **Tasks** lists changes D4H can't take automatically, such as retiring an
  item. Make the change in D4H, then press **Check**, or **Dismiss** the task.

Editing items and **Tasks** need an I3 role, or the Admin or Owner role.

It uses your own personal D4H access token, not one shared by the organisation,
so you see what your D4H account can see. Add a token in your account settings
under **D4H**.

The organisation also needs the D4H integration turned on in **Organisation
Settings**, where an owner or admin turns modules on and off too.
