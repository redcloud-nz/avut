---
title: What's new in AVUT 0.12
---

### A fresh look

Pages now sit on a light tint, with a white sidebar and navigation bar. Anything
you can click turns blue as you point at it, and selected items show in a
stronger blue. On the recording pages, the result you've recorded is circled.

### Session times

A skill check session now has a start time and an optional end, or runs all day
across one or more days. Times are in your organisation's new **Time zone**
setting, under **Organisation Settings** › **General**. An approved session can't
be edited until it's reopened. See
[Create the session](/docs/skill-track/sessions#1-create-the-session).

### Every skill in one place

Skill Track's new **Skills** page lists every skill in the packages you
subscribe to, in one table. Skills and skill groups have their own pages, and
their names link to them throughout Skill Track. See
[Packages](/docs/skill-track/packages#skills).

### Catalogue is now Packages

Skill Track's **Catalogue** is now called **Packages**, split into the packages
you subscribe to and the ones available. If a package's author unpublishes,
archives or deletes it, its skills leave your sessions and reports, and it shows
as **Withdrawn** so you can still unsubscribe. See
[Withdrawn packages](/docs/skill-track/packages#withdrawn-packages).

### Focus a session review

**Show**, in the review page's menu, narrows the Personnel and Skills cards to
just the conflicts or just the excluded checks. The checks dialog has **Include
all** and **Exclude all**. See
[Review and approve](/docs/skill-track/sessions#5-review-and-approve).

### Record a check from an earlier day

**Add Check** has a **Date checked** field, so you can record a result from
before your organisation used AVUT. See
[Checks](/docs/skill-track/checks#adding-a-check-outside-a-session).

### Package history for authors

Skill groups and skills in the Skill Package Builder have their own **History**,
and a package's History now includes changes to its groups and skills.

### A quicker actions menu

Press <Keys combo="mod+." /> (<Keys combo="Ctrl+." /> on Windows) to open the
actions menu of the page you're on. See
[Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/getting-started/controls#keyboard-shortcuts).

### D4H, reorganised

D4H Views is now **D4H**, and I3 is now **D4H I3**. D4H has team-by-team
**Members**, **Equipment Categories** and **Equipment Brands**, and a page for
each item. Owners, admins and the I3 roles can edit an item in D4H from its
page. Every D4H name links to its page. See [D4H](/docs/d4h).

### Return items in D4H I3

D4H I3 now has a **Return** form, and **Return** on an issued item's page. D4H
can't retire items through AVUT, so each return adds a task to the new
[Tasks](/docs/d4h#tasks) list for someone to finish in D4H. Issuing and
returning items email the people your organisation chooses, and send the member
a receipt. See [Returning items](/docs/d4h-i3/returning) and
[Notifications](/docs/d4h-i3#notifications).

### Templates that fit the team

**Issue** only offers templates whose D4H records belong to the recipient's team
or your D4H organisation. Owners and admins can limit new templates to your
organisation's own records. See [Templates](/docs/d4h-i3#templates).

### Safer D4H team sync

Linking a team to D4H now shows you who would be imported before anyone is.
You can untick changes you don't want, and the sync sums up what it did. See
[Linking a team to D4H](/docs/admin/teams#linking-a-team-to-d4h).
