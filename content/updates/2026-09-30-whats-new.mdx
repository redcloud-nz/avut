---
title: Introducing "What's new"
publishedAt: 2026-09-30
description: A short note in AVUT whenever something new arrives.
---

When we add something new to AVUT, you'll now see a short note about it the next
time you sign in. Each note appears once. Choose **Got it** to close it and it
won't come back.

Missed something, or want to read it again? Choose **What's new** at the bottom
of the sidebar to see the most recent updates, or open the full list of updates
in the documentation.
