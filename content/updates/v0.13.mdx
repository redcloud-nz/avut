---
title: What's new in AVUT 0.13
---

### A warmer look

AVUT has a new, warmer colour scheme, with status colours on badges and alerts
so a warning or an error stands out at a glance. Behind every page, a fine dot
texture drifts slowly and follows your pointer.

### Team spaces

A team can now have its own space, for the people who run it. Give a team a
**Slug** in **Admin** › **Teams**, then grant users access on its new **Access**
card. They'll find it under **Teams** in the scope switcher, with the team's
roster on its dashboard and a **Team Admin** area to edit the team, its roster,
who has access and its D4H link. See
[Team spaces](/docs/getting-started/team-spaces).

### Sessions for a team

A skill check session can belong to a team. Pick one on **New Session** and its
active members are added as the session's personnel. The Sessions list has a
**Team** column to filter by. See
[Create the session](/docs/skill-track/sessions#1-create-the-session).

### Sessions over several days

The session **When** picker has **All day** and **Multiple days** switches, so a
timed session can now run across several days, such as a weekend exercise from
Saturday 9:00 to Sunday 15:00. Skill lists in sessions show one section per
skill group, headed "Package · Group", with a count of the skills you've
ticked.

### Search every check

**Checks** now lists every skill check, not just the last month's, with a
**Filters** card to narrow it by person, skill, session, assessor, result and
date. Filters are part of the address, so you can bookmark or share a view. A
skill's page links to its checks as **Recent checks**. See
[Checks](/docs/skill-track/checks#finding-checks).

### Archived skills step aside

When a package's author archives a skill, or its group, the skill leaves your
**Skills** list, the session skill picker and the reports. Checks already
recorded are kept.

### Tidier record pages

Detail pages share one layout: a card for each record, labelled with what it is
(such as "Person", "Linked User" or "D4H Member"), its ID first, and when it was
last updated. A status such as **Archived** or **Withdrawn** shows as a badge
beside the page title, and related lists show as tiles.

### Bundles in D4H I3

A **bundle** is a standard-issue kit list, such as what a recruit should be
issued. Compare one with a D4H team to see which members hold each item and
which are missing it. See [Bundles](/docs/d4h-i3/bundles).

### Spot gaps in issued kit

Templates have a **Brand & Model** setting. **Issued Types** and bundle
comparisons use it, with the template's variants and serial number setting, to
flag items recorded in D4H with missing or wrong details. See
[Checking issued items](/docs/d4h-i3#checking-issued-items).

### See your submitted forms

**Issue** and **Return** have **Drafts** and **Submitted** tabs. Open a
submitted form to see it read-only. A submitted Return also shows the D4H task
queued for each item. The forms now say **Issued to** or **Returned by**, and
**Recorded by**, and you can search the member list by name or team. See
[Issuing items](/docs/d4h-i3/issuing#drafts-and-submitted-forms).

### More from D4H

Equipment models have their own pages, listing their items in every team. Team,
member, category, type, brand and model pages have an **Open in D4H** button,
and every D4H page has a help button. See [D4H](/docs/d4h#open-in-d4h).

### D4H access tokens

You can now edit a D4H access token's label, or replace its value with a new
token from D4H, without removing it. Organisation tokens, under **Admin** ›
**D4H Access Tokens**, also have an **Access** setting: **Read only** or **Read
and write**. See
[D4H Access Tokens](/docs/admin/organization#d4h-access-tokens).

### A Reply-To address for your emails

**Organisation Settings** › **General** has a **Reply-To address**, so replies to
invitations and D4H I3 emails reach someone. See
[General](/docs/admin/organization#general).
