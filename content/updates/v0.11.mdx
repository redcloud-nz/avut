---
title: What's new in AVUT 0.11
---

### Introducing "What's new"

Each time a new version of AVUT comes out, you'll now see a short note about
what has changed the next time you sign in. Each note appears once. Choose
**Got it** to close it and it won't come back.

Missed something, or want to read it again? Select the version number at the
bottom of the sidebar, or choose **What's new** in your account menu, to see the
notes for the most recent versions. You can also open the full list of updates
in the documentation.

### Review and approve skill check sessions

A session now has a proper finish. Its **Review** page sums up what was
recorded, lets a Skills Admin leave out any checks that shouldn't count, and
ends with **Approve**. Only checks in an approved session count in the reports.
Approving locks the session; a Skills Admin can **Reopen** it if something needs
changing. See [Review and approve](/docs/skill-track/sessions#5-review-and-approve).

### Several assessors in one session

A session can now have several assessors recording at once. Each sees their own
checks, and a row says **Also checked by** when someone else has already
checked that person and skill, updating as they go. Where two assessors both
checked the same thing, the review page asks which check counts. See
[Working with other assessors](/docs/skill-track/sessions#working-with-other-assessors).

### Quicker recording

Each row on the recording pages now has **Fail** and **Pass** buttons that save
with one tap, and **More** for every other result, a note and the skill's
description. **Options** gathers the recording view, the skill order and the
session's personnel, skills and assessors in one place. See
[Record results](/docs/skill-track/sessions#4-record-results).

### Notes

The new Notes module is a shared notebook for your organisation, and your
account has personal notes that only you can see. See [Notes](/docs/notes).

### Help for the screen you're on

The help button at the top right of each page now opens a short note about that
screen, with a link to the full guide for more.

### A Rubbish bin for deleted records

Deleting personnel, teams, team memberships, I3 templates or skill packages now
moves them to the **Rubbish** bin, where they can be recovered. They're removed
for good after 30 days, or however long your organisation chooses. See
[Rubbish Bin](/docs/admin/rubbish-bin).

### See every change to a record

People, teams, skill packages and skill check sessions now have a **History**
page, listing every change, who made it and when.

### Clearer roles

Roles are now one list you can combine freely, with new **Skills Admin**,
**Skills Reporter** and **I3 Admin** roles, and ownership is granted with **Make
owner** instead of being picked as a role. Owners, admins and Skills Admins can
hand out the Skills Assessor role from Skill Track's new **Assessors** page. See
[Roles](/docs/admin#roles).

### Organisation Settings, on their own page

**Organisation Settings** now has its own page in Admin, where everyone can see
how the organisation is set up and owners and admins change settings in place.
See [Organisation](/docs/admin/organization#organisation-settings).

### Your settings, in your menu

Your **Account**, **Organisations**, **D4H** and **Preferences** pages are now in
the menu under your name. Every date in AVUT follows your date format, time
format and time zone preferences, and you can close your own account from the
**Account** page. See [Your Account](/docs/account).
