## v0.13.2 (Nucula)

Small patch: a public form asking Christchurch members whether they'd use a team soft shell jacket, and the start of a way to store answers from people without an AVUT account.

### Highlights

- **Soft shell jacket form** — a no-login page at `/forms/chch-soft-shell-jacket`: photos of the Biz Collection Geneva J307 in Men's and Ladies' fits (front and back, with the standard NZRT branding), its features and size chart, then team, name, "Would you use one?" with fit and size, and comments.
- **Answers from people without an account** — public form answers are stored in the owning organisation as submitted forms and logged, and its admins can download them as CSV (`/orgs/chch-cdem/admin/forms/chch-soft-shell-jacket/answers`). New forms register in `src/lib/public-forms.ts`.
- **Bot protection** — public form submissions are checked with Vercel BotID.
