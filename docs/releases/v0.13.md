### The name

RFA Nucula was a fleet oiler of the Royal Fleet Auxiliary that served with the New Zealand Division from 1924 to 1937. Armstrong Whitworth built her on the Tyne in 1906 as the tanker _Hermione_. She carried oil across the Pacific as the Japanese _Soyo Maru_, then came back under the British flag as a fleet oiler in the First World War, named, like Shell's tankers, after a seashell. The Admiralty lent her to New Zealand in May 1924, when the oil-burning cruisers Dunedin and Diomede replaced the coal-burning Chatham, because the station's ports had coal but no fuel oil. She spent the next thirteen years carrying fuel oil from California to the naval base at Devonport, and met the cruisers at island anchorages such as Suva and Apia to refuel them on their Pacific cruises. Her last voyage ended in June 1937, after which she was a storage hulk in the Waitematā Harbour through the Second World War. She was scuttled off Cuvier Island on 24 October 1947.
