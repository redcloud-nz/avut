## v0.13 (Nucula)

Teams get their own spaces, with access grants and a Team Admin area, and skill check sessions can belong to a team. D4H I3 gains bundles and checks on issued kit, the new D4H Attendance Reminders module chases unanswered exercise invitations, and AVUT has a warmer look.

### Highlights

- **A warmer look** — an espresso and caramel palette replaces the teal, with status colours on badges and alerts, one shadow and corner-radius scale, and new chart palettes.
- **Team spaces** — give a team a slug and grant users access, and they get its own space under Teams in the scope switcher: the team's roster on its dashboard, and a Team Admin area to edit the team, its roster, who has access and its D4H link.
- **Sessions for a team** — a skill check session can belong to a team, which adds its active members as the session's personnel. The Sessions list has a Team column to filter by.
- **Sessions over several days** — the When picker has All day and Multiple days switches, and session skill lists show one "Package · Group" section per skill group.
- **Search every check** — Checks lists every skill check, not just the last month's, with filters for person, skill, session, assessor, result and date that stay in the address. A skill's page links to its recent checks.
- **Archived skills step aside** — archiving a skill or its group takes it out of Skills, the session skill picker and the reports. Recorded checks are kept.
- **D4H Attendance Reminders** — a new module that emails team members who haven't answered a D4H exercise invitation, on a schedule set team by team. It uses the organisation's D4H token, and members can opt out.
- **Bundles in D4H I3** — standard-issue kit lists, compared with a D4H team to show which members hold each item and which are missing it.
- **Spot gaps in issued kit** — templates gain a Brand & Model setting, and Issued Types and bundle comparisons flag items recorded in D4H with missing or wrong details.
- **Submitted forms** — Issue and Return have Drafts and Submitted tabs. A submitted Return shows the D4H task queued for each item.
- **More from D4H** — equipment model pages, Open in D4H on team, member, category, type, brand and model pages, and a help button on every D4H page.
- **D4H access tokens** — edit a token's label or replace its value without removing it. Organisation tokens can be Read only or Read and write.
- **Tidier record pages** — every detail page uses one record-card shape, and shows a status such as Archived as a badge beside the title.
- **A Reply-To address** — organisations can set one, so replies to their AVUT emails reach someone.
- **Security** — Next.js 16.4.6 and better-auth 1.7.26, for security advisories.

### The name

RFA Nucula was a fleet oiler of the Royal Fleet Auxiliary that served with the New Zealand Division from 1924 to 1937. Armstrong Whitworth built her on the Tyne in 1906 as the tanker _Hermione_. She carried oil across the Pacific as the Japanese _Soyo Maru_, then came back under the British flag as a fleet oiler in the First World War, named, like Shell's tankers, after a seashell. The Admiralty lent her to New Zealand in May 1924, when the oil-burning cruisers Dunedin and Diomede replaced the coal-burning Chatham, because the station's ports had coal but no fuel oil. She spent the next thirteen years carrying fuel oil from California to the naval base at Devonport, and met the cruisers at island anchorages such as Suva and Apia to refuel them on their Pacific cruises. Her last voyage ended in June 1937, after which she was a storage hulk in the Waitematā Harbour through the Second World War. She was scuttled off Cuvier Island on 24 October 1947.
