## v0.13.1 (Nucula)

Small patch: a public demo of the kit-check form, so team leaders can see what their members would be sent before anyone is emailed.

### Highlights

- **Kit-check form demo** — a no-login page at `/forms/kit-check/demo` showing the equipment check each member would get, filled with made-up data: what D4H says they have, what they should have, things only some members hold, an item the team is considering (with its size chart), and anything else. Nothing entered is saved or sent; submitting shows what the equipment officer would receive. (#577)
- **`/forms`** — a new top-level home for forms opened from an emailed link, open without signing in.
