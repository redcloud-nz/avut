## v0.11 (Laburnum)

Skill check sessions get a proper finish, with review, conflict resolution and approval, and several assessors can now record in one session at once. Alongside that come the Notes module, a Rubbish bin for deleted records, per-record history, clearer roles, and in-app help and "What's new".

### Highlights

- **Session review and approval** — a review page with summary tiles, Personnel and Skills coverage cards, and a Conflicts card. A Skills Admin excludes checks, resolves conflicts and approves. Approval locks the session, a Skills Admin can reopen it, and only approved checks count in reports.
- **Several assessors per session** — assessors are chosen per session, and each recording row shows **Also checked by** for other assessors' checks, polled live while the page is open.
- **Quicker recording** — one-tap Fail and Pass on every row, a **More** dialog for the other results, a note and the skill description, and a Recording Options sheet for the recording mode, skill order and the session's personnel, skills and assessors.
- **Notes** — a shared notebook for each organisation and personal notes for each account, on a new master-detail layout, with edit history.
- **Rubbish bin** — deleted personnel, teams, memberships, I3 templates and skill packages are recoverable until purged, after a retention period the organisation sets (30 days by default). Accounts can be closed and restored the same way.
- **History** — people, teams, skill packages, skill check sessions and D4H tokens have a page listing every change, who made it and when.
- **Clearer roles** — roles combine freely, with new Skills Admin, Skills Reporter and I3 Admin roles. Ownership is granted with Make owner, and Skills Admins can hand out the Skills Assessor role.
- **Help and What's new** — the help button opens a short card for the current screen linking to the full guide, the end-user guides are rewritten for this release, and a "What's new" note appears once per release.
- **Settings** — Organisation Settings has its own page with in-place editing. Account, Organisations, D4H and Preferences moved into the user menu, and every date follows the viewer's date format, time format and time zone.
- **Developer workflow** — a fixed dev-server port per checkout, `/avut-explore`, `/avut-ship`, `/avut-docs` and `/avut-status`, and scripted milestone and docs-issue handling.

### The name

HMS Laburnum was an Acacia-class sloop that served with the New Zealand Division from 1922 to 1935. Built in Glasgow in 1915, she spent the First World War on anti-submarine work, and went to the aid of the torpedoed liner _Hesperian_ in September 1915. In New Zealand she toured the country's ports and made an annual cruise of the Pacific Islands, carrying the Administrator of Western Samoa to the Tokelau Islands on one of them. She left Auckland in 1935 to become a drill ship for the Straits Settlements naval volunteer reserve at Singapore, and was scuttled there when Singapore fell in 1942.
