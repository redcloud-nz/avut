# Version Names

Proposed codenames for AVUT releases. Pick the next unused name from the top of
the list.

## Theme 1 — Ships of the New Zealand Division of the Royal Navy

The [New Zealand Division of the Royal Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Division_of_the_Royal_Navy)
existed from 1921 until 1941, when it became the Royal New Zealand Navy. These
are the vessels that served with it, listed in order of when they entered New
Zealand service.

| Name         | Type                              | NZ service | Notes                                                                                                                          |
| ------------ | --------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `veronica`   | Acacia-class sloop                | 1920–1934  | One of the first vessels of the new division. Later famous for the 1931 Napier earthquake relief effort ("the Veronica code"). |
| `chatham`    | Town-class cruiser                | 1920–1924  | Early flagship of the division, on loan from the Royal Navy.                                                                   |
| `torch`      | Convoy sloop                      | 1921–1924  | Short-serving early sloop of the division.                                                                                     |
| `philomel`   | Pearl-class cruiser               | 1921–1941  | The division's depot/training ship for its entire existence; the name lives on as the Devonport naval base, HMNZS Philomel.    |
| `laburnum`   | Acacia-class sloop                | 1922–1935  | Auckland-based drill ship for the Royal Naval Volunteer Reserve.                                                               |
| `dunedin`    | Danae-class cruiser               | 1924–1937  | One of the two cruisers that replaced Chatham; sunk by U-124 in 1941 while in RN service.                                      |
| `nucula`     | Fleet oiler (RFA)                 | 1924–1937  | The division's dedicated oil tanker.                                                                                           |
| `diomede`    | Danae-class cruiser               | 1926–1935  | Sister ship to Dunedin; the pair formed the division's cruiser strength through the late 1920s.                                |
| `wakakura`   | Castle-class minesweeping trawler | 1926–1941  | The division's first locally crewed warship and training tender; Māori name.                                                   |
| `leith`      | Grimsby-class sloop               | 1934–1939  | Escort sloop; returned to the Royal Navy at the outbreak of war.                                                               |
| `wellington` | Grimsby-class sloop               | 1935–1947  | Served through WWII; the RN sloop preserved on the Thames Embankment in London as HQS Wellington.                              |
| `achilles`   | Leander-class cruiser             | 1936–1941  | Fought at the Battle of the River Plate (1939) against the _Admiral Graf Spee_ — the division's most celebrated action.        |
| `leander`    | Leander-class cruiser             | 1937–1941  | Lead ship of her class; sank the Italian raider _Ramb I_ in 1941. Continued with the RNZN after 1941.                          |
| `auckland`   | Egret-class sloop                 | 1938–1939  | Briefly with the division before RN service; sunk off Tobruk in 1941.                                                          |
| `puriri`     | Requisitioned coastal minesweeper | 1941       | Struck a mine and sank in the Hauraki Gulf in 1941 — one of the first NZ naval losses of the war. Māori name (a native tree).  |

## Theme 2 — Ships of the Royal New Zealand Navy

Vessels of the [Royal New Zealand Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ships_of_the_Royal_New_Zealand_Navy)
from its founding in 1941 onward. A curated selection of the named ships — the
many numbered wartime launches, trawlers and minesweepers are omitted.
`achilles`, `leander` and `wellington` also appear in Theme 1 (they carried over
from the Division, or the name was reused).

| Name            | Type                             | Service      | Notes                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `gambia`        | Crown Colony-class cruiser       | 1943–1946    | On loan from the RN; present in Tokyo Bay for the Japanese surrender in September 1945.                                                                          |
| `arabis`        | Flower-class corvette            | 1944–1948    | One of two Flower-class corvettes loaned for late-war escort work.                                                                                               |
| `arbutus`       | Flower-class corvette            | 1944–1948    | Sister to Arabis.                                                                                                                                                |
| `bellona`       | Dido-class cruiser               | 1946–1956    | Post-war light cruiser, loaned from the RN.                                                                                                                      |
| `black-prince`  | Dido-class cruiser               | 1946–1961    | Served as an RNZN cruiser for 15 years, including Korean War deployments.                                                                                        |
| `hawea`         | Loch-class frigate               | 1948–1965    | One of six Loch-class frigates that formed the post-war escort force; Korean War service. Name reused for a Lake-class patrol craft and again for a current IPV. |
| `kaniere`       | Loch-class frigate               | 1948–1967    | Korean War service.                                                                                                                                              |
| `pukaki`        | Loch-class frigate               | 1948–1966    | Korean War service; name later reused for patrol craft.                                                                                                          |
| `rotoiti`       | Loch-class frigate               | 1948–1967    | Korean War service; name later reused for patrol craft.                                                                                                          |
| `taupo`         | Loch-class frigate               | 1948–1962    | Name later reused for a Lake-class then a Protector-class IPV.                                                                                                   |
| `tutira`        | Loch-class frigate               | 1948–1951    | Shortest-serving of the Loch-class frigates.                                                                                                                     |
| `royalist`      | Dido-class cruiser               | 1956–1966    | Extensively modernised anti-aircraft cruiser; RNZN flagship through the late 1950s and early 1960s.                                                              |
| `otago`         | Rothesay-class (Type 12) frigate | 1960–1983    | Sent to protest French nuclear testing at Mururoa in 1973 with a NZ cabinet minister aboard. Name reused for a current OPV.                                      |
| `taranaki`      | Rothesay-class (Type 12) frigate | 1961–1982    | Half of the RNZN's Type 12 frigate pair.                                                                                                                         |
| `waikato`       | Leander-class frigate            | 1966–1998    | First RNZN frigate built new for New Zealand and first with a helicopter hangar.                                                                                 |
| `tui`           | Oceanographic research ship      | 1970–1997    | US-built research vessel operated for oceanographic and acoustic work.                                                                                           |
| `canterbury`    | Leander-class frigate            | 1971–2005    | Last of the RNZN's Leander frigates in service; name reused for the current multi-role vessel.                                                                   |
| `monowai`       | Hydrographic survey ship         | 1977–1997    | Converted passenger ferry; the navy's dedicated survey ship for two decades.                                                                                     |
| `kiwi`          | Moa-class inshore patrol craft   | 1983–2007    | Revived a WWII name; the wartime Kiwi rammed the submarine I-1 off Guadalcanal in 1943.                                                                          |
| `southland`     | Leander-class frigate            | 1983–1995    | Ex-Royal Navy Leander (HMS Dido) transferred to the RNZN.                                                                                                        |
| `moa`           | Moa-class inshore patrol craft   | 1984–2007    | Revived a WWII Bird-class name (the wartime Moa helped sink the Japanese submarine I-1 in the same 1943 action).                                                 |
| `hinau`         | Moa-class inshore patrol craft   | 1985–2007    | Named after a native tree.                                                                                                                                       |
| `endeavour`     | Fleet replenishment tanker       | 1988–2017    | Third RNZN ship of the name; fleet oiler and regular Antarctic resupply vessel.                                                                                  |
| `charles-upham` | Sealift / logistics ship         | 1995–1998    | Named for the double-VC recipient; poor sea-keeping cut its RNZN career short.                                                                                   |
| `te-kaha`       | Anzac-class frigate              | 1997–present | One of the RNZN's two front-line frigates; te reo name meaning "fighting prowess".                                                                               |
| `resolution`    | Survey / research ship           | 1997–2012    | Ex-US Navy survey ship; carried on the Cook-era naming tradition.                                                                                                |
| `te-mana`       | Anzac-class frigate              | 1999–present | Sister to Te Kaha; name meaning "prestige / authority".                                                                                                          |
| `wellington`    | Protector-class OPV              | 2007–present | Offshore patrol vessel; latest RNZN ship of the name.                                                                                                            |
| `canterbury`    | Multi-role vessel                | 2007–present | Sealift and amphibious-support ship; the RNZN's largest-capacity vessel before Aotearoa.                                                                         |
| `manawanui`     | Dive and hydrographic support    | 2019–2024    | Ran aground and sank off Samoa in October 2024 — the first RNZN loss of a ship at sea since WWII. Name means "steadfast".                                        |
| `aotearoa`      | Polar replenishment tanker       | 2020–present | The largest ship ever operated by the RNZN; ice-strengthened for Antarctic replenishment.                                                                        |
